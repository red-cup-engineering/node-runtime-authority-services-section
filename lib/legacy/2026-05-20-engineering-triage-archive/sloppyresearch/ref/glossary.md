### Minimal Vocabulary

is  
coherence of a stratum within a category.

stratum  
a condition localized within a category, participating in coherence.

path  
a directed relation linking two strata within a category.

cell  
a bounded coherence formed by related strata and paths.

category  
a coherence of multiple cells composing a field.

field  
an encompassing coherence integrating categories into a larger cell.

from  
the originating stratum of a path within a cell.

to  
the target stratum of a path within a cell.

adjacent  
a stratum sharing a boundary with another stratum in a cell.

extends  
a path continuing coherence from one stratum into another.

derives  
a path emerging from an existing coherence toward a new one.

folds  
a path returning from a stratum into its own cell to sustain coherence.

reflects  
a path relating a stratum to itself within a cell.

pushes  
a directed path transmitting condition upward through strata.

pulls  
a directed path transmitting condition downward through strata.

boundary  
a stratum marking difference between adjacent strata.

complement  
a stratum exterior to a boundary within a cell’s coherence.

quotient  
a coherence identifying multiple strata as a single stratum.

difference  
a condition generating a boundary between strata.

coherence  
a stable condition of compatible relations among strata within a cell.

colimit  
a coherence arising from the convergence of multiple paths in a cell.

limit  
a coherence arising from mutual constraint among paths in a cell.

condition  
a local coherence stabilized on a stratum within a category.

resides  
a path locating a condition within its stratum.

dimension  
a condition expressing the order of a stratum within a cell.

connects  
a path forming coherence between two strata.

relates  
a path establishing connection between any two strata within a category.

includes  
a relation where one cell contains another within a category.

excludes  
a relation where one cell differentiates from another through a boundary.

realizes  
a condition instantiated through coherence of a path or cell.

represents  
a cell expressing the coherence of another cell within a category.

projects  
a path emitting a reduced condition from one cell into another.

transfers  
a path conveying condition between strata within a category.

contains  
a relation where a field holds or encloses a category.

encloses  
a relation where a category bounds a cell within a field.

composes  
a relation joining cells into a category through coherence.

integrates  
a relation joining categories into a field through coherence.

interfaces  
a stratum serving as a shared boundary between two cells.

mediates  
a condition maintaining coherence between related strata.

recurs  
a path through which a cell reappears within its field.

emerges  
a path by which a new stratum differentiates within a field.

dissolves  
a path by which a coherence ceases within a field.

asserts  
a condition affirming coherence of a relation.

denies  
a condition negating coherence of a relation.

contrasts  
a condition placing two strata in differential coherence.

conditions  
a condition delimiting possibility for coherence within a field.

grounds  
a condition providing foundational coherence for another condition.

stabilizes  
a condition reinforcing coherence of a cell.

perturbs  
a condition disrupting coherence of a cell.
