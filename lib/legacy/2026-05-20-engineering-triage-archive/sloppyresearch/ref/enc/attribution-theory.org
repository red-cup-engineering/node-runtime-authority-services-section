#+title: attribution theory
#+date: [2025-08-27 Wed 06:38]
#+index: attribution theory

* Lineage

In [[id:10f62117-6239-4578-ab57-fe3bfeea56ee][1958]], [[id:1eb71e38-6720-4fab-9ff6-d501cdb1105b][Fritz Heider]] [[id:22a32b15-f24a-4d4b-89c1-4233b7cc3856][posited]] that [[id:6882cc6d-049d-4c68-866a-67bc57fce725][humans]] act as [[id:480dd22f-d4dc-4540-94e9-91243de24787][naïve scientists]], [[id:bbb0df8c-b366-4821-a552-1407280c6d9a][doing]] [[id:7f50cace-4696-455f-8cfd-b485c4b22872][causal analysis]] on the [[id:522bc8d5-bc22-405a-80cf-485768e56cae][human behavior]] they [[id:046e0943-cea7-451f-9c3e-38d5ffb034b5][experience]]. In Heider's analysis, attribution assigned beahvior to either [[id:726d4c2b-7a7d-4dfc-b6b1-2a781639a07c][dispositional cause]] or [[id:689cfe1f-292c-4f47-868f-b18a04c823eb][situational cause]]. This [[id:c9cc4a84-0f4a-4bd6-8ebe-5048131187bc][bifurcated classification]] formalized [[id:aa700898-32d6-4e4d-9ea3-f711a246aeea][attributing]] as [[id:24540b59-852d-46f7-82f2-29814d7e8f2a][conditioning]] an [[id:32e00173-c695-4bb6-b0d2-9dc17fec128b][act]] within a causal schema.

In [[id:125f4df7-186e-4129-b3fd-3cd10af183c6][1965]], [[id:38500867-5885-4574-a950-348f24306287][Edward E. Jones]] and [[id:7afbfde9-86d3-41da-9ef1-9e8a36d66c3a][Keith Davis]] [[id:f7cc4bac-8a9d-43a2-a76f-4e014ae70405][authored]] [[id:52d0ce2c-62cf-4f08-8444-3cdfba15a8e8][Correspondent Inference Theory]], which refined Heider's model by focusing on [[id:bcebbe83-6580-4728-89e9-c8f0eeeb27b5][intentionality]]. In their analysis, attributing occurs when [[id:65da32b3-d07c-493e-80e7-05b8d06c60e8][observers]] [[id:42b47a03-af7c-4e10-850f-9fba06696c21][infer]] that a behavior [[id:9f8b2529-479c-4f6f-9da8-1b5217e891b7][correlates]] with a stable trait in the actor.
