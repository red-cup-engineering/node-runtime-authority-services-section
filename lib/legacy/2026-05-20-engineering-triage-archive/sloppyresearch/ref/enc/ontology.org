An */ontology/* is a [[id:fb63f9e1-b14d-4745-bda3-b018ecd864d5][theory]] of [[id:7706e59a-4dca-436b-a67c-66a45e9eca8d][being]].

In [[id:7031b7c2-7bfd-410e-8ed7-fbfc81dc3afc][relational dynamics]], an ontology is one or more [[id:c957d6cf-a884-420e-964e-cdcec4ff3b7d][claim of relation]], which may be understood as [[id:43bba717-cfa8-4f77-9079-76c00fd9f586][secondary intension]] [[id:290fb2b6-4f18-4eba-bf0c-9f107e395005][expressed]] as a [[id:4afb18b4-a9be-4df6-88bc-ec387c4c4405][Fregean first-order predicate]] to establish a [[id:71545c4c-d8e6-4b60-830e-92bd261ba31d][Tarskian model-theoretic truth]].
