*/Neue Sachlichkeit/* ([[id:9866885a-2569-435b-96cc-f96ec788ec18][English]]: "New Objectivity") was a [[id:6214a535-4757-44b5-b0cb-bba684ee6626][cultural]] and [[id:4ac1f34f-5728-4a16-9a36-91bb056f40c4][art movement]] in [[id:47126060-f5bb-4e8c-a313-973869d7cff9][Germany]] in the [[id:7bb796bb-27fc-4075-8a5d-ade8363bbfcc][1920s]] and early [[id:b5ebb0be-c29f-4981-b74e-da7327e6bd0d][1930s]] that rejected [[id:814127a6-88ca-4245-9c51-7b8d387d6144][Expressionism]].

Itself was divided into [[id:aa9a4967-f967-4683-bd5a-340a82317e10][socially critiquing]] Verists and the non-critiquing Classicists.
