#+title: Old English language
#+date [2025-08-12 Tue 05:50]
#+index: language!Old English
#+index: Old English!language

The */Old English language/* was a [[id:e8bfbd06-21f6-4e9a-a42b-27a93d5c7b21][language]] used by the [[id:60e4b941-7c0f-4fac-b78f-36efa1ec198f][Anglo-Saxon people]] in what is now [[id:8ad72df7-1bff-476c-bc8a-29b43ec55b1b][England]] and [[id:f7eec1d7-3476-49bf-afb6-9909ace61218][southern Scotland]] from roughly [[id:bee559cb-ee42-4129-957c-0d760c5d8869][500ce]] to the [[id:3caf58bb-986e-4e8c-a251-a5843c5f7da4][Norman Conquest]]. It's the earliest form of [[id:9866885a-2569-435b-96cc-f96ec788ec18][English language]].

Its roots are in the [[id:38574822-35ee-4bcb-b3b8-25ce7ddf504d][West Germanic]] branch of the [[id:0a97a066-09bf-4f10-b7eb-9b937f89da78][Indo-European languages]], [[id:bc03e36a-a423-4e79-a9ec-f6909b7df428][developing]] from the [[id:69b9acad-ed82-4918-b211-dcad07a5b551][Anglo-Frisian language]] of the [[id:699948b4-8254-470b-8946-eca6847d4781][Angles]], [[id:6ac8c213-0256-4858-8854-d8d21535c3f6][Saxons]], and [[id:69849767-bfe7-4bfe-820a-1a04bff8d550][Jutes]].

*Old English* was a collection of regional dialects:

- [[id:a9509929-bff7-431c-bf23-eac75907cabc][West Saxon]] was spoken in [[id:5e07f8c8-dfd1-40ae-b1aa-9715f1419f0b][southern England]] and became the literary standard.
- [[id:2b823923-1428-4aaf-ab5a-4383b9cbf802][Mercian]] was spoken in [[id:62b1c12d-02f8-4ab5-bb68-40268208c660][central England]]
- [[id:be293ceb-3786-446d-8b95-a510faec1e29][Northumbrian]] was spoken in [[id:4894db24-1998-4e43-bc34-9b3b86d93716][northern England]] and [[id:f7eec1d7-3476-49bf-afb6-9909ace61218][southern Scotland]]
- [[id:13385238-45a3-4452-9402-b1c73e67e99c][Kentish]] was spoken in [[id:ee9f0585-44f3-429a-bc55-ce146f4b6327][southeastern England]]

Originally, *Old English* was written with [[id:f83531ad-c323-4cbb-bc5a-7fe5854fd44c][Runic script]], but transitioned to the [[id:189e2b39-fee7-4868-978f-6aadde28a57b][Latin alphabet]].

The most famous work in *Old English* is [[id:6fd8366e-f5fb-414d-8c14-ce57827194aa][Beowulf]].
