An */ontological process/* is a [[id:4a53867b-afa3-437b-b979-385597e0e616][process]] that [[id:bbb0df8c-b366-4821-a552-1407280c6d9a][does]] [[id:7ce83293-bb4b-4787-953b-5d8b7506cc78][ontologizing]]. An ontological process is a form of [[id:f6dbe2e2-b75e-448a-85ad-0e75c938ff53][conceptual analysis]] where the [[id:fd6ecbed-9bdf-4570-b4b2-5ff7744c0a96][concept]] being [[id:9ca86d85-debc-432f-b882-bbffe32a826b][analyzed]] is [[id:0bf0b37a-1181-4a1f-a036-93d7b397eb52][related]] to [[id:7706e59a-4dca-436b-a67c-66a45e9eca8d][being]] or [[id:d7649d6a-66cc-4efb-b20f-5f2b960bcab8][knowing]] about being.
