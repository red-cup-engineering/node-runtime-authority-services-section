#+title: invisibilizing
#+date: [2025-08-24 Sun 09:02]
#+index: invisibilizing

*/Invizibilizing/* is an [[id:32e00173-c695-4bb6-b0d2-9dc17fec128b][act]] [[id:455ea21f-b9e3-4299-a294-5709f8195e05][resulting]] in a [[id:e5248432-6d1e-436e-9bdc-50fe0fcd3fe6][thing]] [[id:7706e59a-4dca-436b-a67c-66a45e9eca8d][being]] [[id:657037a0-7cbb-4b1b-bc4b-4756ce4fecc7][invisible]].

When the result of an act includes a thing becoming invisible, that act can be said to be invisibilizing that thing.

The term is often used in [[id:bad397e9-7897-48b7-9046-5cc298419fea][sociology]], and [[id:9bb6c2a7-bb47-4bc0-aa89-cee60297edca][critical theory]], especially [[id:edf99d69-953a-4249-afa2-625872d78dd6][criticism of tradition]], [[id:c1fbe4ee-6adf-4e41-a48b-63cd76441136][criticism of liberalism]].
