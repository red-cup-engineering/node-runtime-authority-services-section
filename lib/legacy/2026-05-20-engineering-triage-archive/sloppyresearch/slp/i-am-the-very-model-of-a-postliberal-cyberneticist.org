#+title: I Am The Very Model of a Postliberal Cyberist
#+date: [2025-10-02 Thu 08:26]

One lovely type of satire is of the lyrical variety, where the parodist lets someone with some real skill do all the hard work of making a melody, meter, and rhyme, and then simply substitutes in their own message. Thankfully this form of laziness is protected as "fair use," so I can steal from Gilbert and Sullivan's Pirates of Penzance, to give you:

"I Am The Very Model of a Postliberal Cyberist"

Verse 1:
I am the very model of a postliberal cyberist
My reflex arcs are optimized with closures that persist
I know the loops of feedback and the thresholds of stability
And calculate conditions with reflexive probability.

I'm very well acquainted too with neuroreflex symmetry,
I chart the ways anticipation governs all affinity.
In short, in matters negative or non-determinable,
I am the very model of a postliberal relational.

Verse 2:
I can derive inclusion from the result of cohering acts,
And demonstrate reflexivity through diagrams of recursive tracks.
I know my self is just a phase of field consistency,
And entropy's the price we pay for existential consistency

I program flows in circuits that are more than mere mechanical:
I map the nodes of being along trajectories autonomical.
In short, in matters with monadic and reductive twists
I am the very model of a postliberal cyberist

Verse 3:
In fact, when I can simulate your subject as a boundary phase
And regulate your neurons by a clever feedback-learning maze,
When I can turn your synapse to a system for anticipation,
And every arc you trace is coded in negation:

When I can prove reflexive form is neither sovereign nor free,
But structured through conditions of recursive circuitry:
Then you will say, in matters neuroreflexivity and such,
That I am the very model of a postliberal cyberist!

Verse 4
When feedback loops are nested in reflexive oscillation,
The closure of a system breeds a fresh destabilization,
Recursion breeds recursion breeds recursion in return -
And the arc becomes the act becomes the art that arcs to burn...

The boundary negates the field, the field rnegates the act,
The unit is a process which the process must subtract
And the sign of stabilization is itself a subtle ruse
So the cyberist singer starts to - buffer - stutter -
- loop - and - loop - and.

Excuse me, I guess we haven't found how this one ends, yet.
