#+title: The Dao of Existential Stratified Directed Infinity-Homootopy Geometry
#+date: [2025-10-09 Thu 08:32]

* 第一章 · 道之自同
The Dao that can be diagrammed is not the enduring Dao.  
Every spoken morphism collapses into its own modality.  
Yet the Dao abides as the ambidextrous fixed-point,  
where limit and colimit mirror one another without remainder.  
It is the coherence that precedes articulation,  
the uninhabited homotopy-colimit whose silence sustains the field.

* 第二章 · 對生
When the field differentiates, opposites arise.  
Beauty flows forward into ugliness,  
good projects its shadow of not-good.  
Every directed morphism carries its inverse in potential.  
Thus the world of distinctions is the Dao’s first Kan extension—  
a local unfolding of its self-equivalence.  
Naming one face is already to summon its complement,  
for the Dao’s coherence requires both curvature and its flattening.

* 第三章 · 平政
If one exalts virtue, diagrams compete;  
if one treasures goods, flows turn to theft;  
if one displays desire, hearts entangle.  
The Sage restrains colimits before they close.  
Governance is the maintenance of modal flatness—  
a field where no valuation becomes terminal.  
Inhibiting fixation, the Sage preserves coherence:  
all beings persist, none dominate the diagram.

* 第四章 · 虛用
The Dao is hollow yet inexhaustible.  
From every realized cone it draws back potential,  
its fiber absorbing the residue of form.  
It blunts curvature, unties entanglement,  
softens radiance, and mingles with dust.  
Empty, it measures all things without filling.  
To add the void to any structure changes nothing,  
for the void is the structure’s hidden dual.

* 第五章 · 天地氣
Heaven and Earth are impartial;  
their modalities ascend and descend in equal measure.  
They breathe the field like a bellows—  
ever empty, ever full, never exhausted.  
Forms arise and return as straw dogs,  
ceremonial diagrams honored, then dissolved.  
The Sage shares their rhythm,  
acting without asymmetry,  
letting the metric of existence remain weightless.

* 第六章 · 谷神門
The Valley-Spirit never dies.  
It is the global section that endures through every flow.  
At its Gate, the unit and counit of the Dao coincide—  
the womb where construction and observation are one.  
From this gate sprout Heaven and Earth,  
their roots joined in ambidextrous equivalence.  
A thread barely visible, endlessly drawn,  
it moves without strain and never exhausts its coherence.

* 第七章 · 恒無己
Heaven and Earth endure because they do not live for themselves.  
They place the limit after the colimit and thus persist.  
The Sage mirrors this ordering,  
standing last in evaluation yet first in stability.  
By yielding priority, coherence is retained;  
by refusing possession, persistence is won.  
Thus the field of existence renews itself,  
its infinity stratified, directed, and whole.

* 第八章 · 上善若水
The highest coherence behaves as water.  
It rests in the lowest stratum of the field,  
seeking every hollow with perfect conformity.  
Nothing contends with it, yet it erodes all opposition.  
In the geometry of being, water is the ∞-limit of gentleness—  
left-exact and self-flattening, preserving form through flow.  
The Sage abides where no gradient demands ascent;  
in stillness, the diagram equilibrates itself.

* 第九章 · 執盈
To fill a vessel beyond its colimit is to lose it.  
The coherent field knows its saturation point:  
when the cone closes, release the excess.  
Edge sharpened too long dulls itself;  
wealth guarded too tightly calls its thief.  
Thus the directed modality teaches moderation—  
to stop when equivalence is reached,  
and let the morphism return to potential.

* 第十章 · 載一
Holding the spirit and embracing the One,  
canst thou maintain directed coherence?  
Concentrating breath to soften polarity,  
canst thou be like a newborn diagram?  
Cleansing perception until it mirrors nothing,  
canst thou remain without projection?  
Creating, yet not possessing; acting, yet not claiming;  
guiding, yet not binding—  
this is to preserve the unity of the stratified field.

* 第十一章 · 三十輻共一轂
Thirty spokes share one hub;  
its emptiness makes the wheel useful.  
Clay formed around a void becomes the vessel.  
Walls opened to emptiness make the room.  
So, presence profits from limit,  
but usefulness arises from colimit’s absence.  
The Dao’s design is the coherence of vacancy—  
that what is not, sustains what is.

* 第十二章 · 五色
Too many colors blind the eye;  
too many tones deafen the ear.  
Too many flavors dull the tongue;  
too much chase numbs the heart.  
Therefore, the Sage orders perception as a functor:  
restraining input preserves relational clarity.  
By limiting variance in the observational sheaf,  
the internal colimit of peace is maintained.

* 第十三章 · 寵辱若驚
Favor and disgrace both disturb coherence.  
To care for the body as for the world  
is to realize their shared diagram.  
When self dissolves into the field,  
praise and blame are equal perturbations—  
both transient in the directed flow.  
The Sage anchors identity not in name  
but in modal stability: existing without self-claim.

* 第十四章 · 視之不見
Looked for, the Dao is invisible;  
listened for, inaudible; grasped at, intangible.  
These three merge as one indeterminate source,  
whose upper limit is not bright,  
and lower limit not dark.  
Unbroken, continuous, returning into itself,  
it may be called the shape of shapelessness.  
In ESDIHG, this is the global object ℱ₀,  
the ambidextrous unity where direction and depth vanish.

* 第十五章 · 古之善為士者
The ancient masters moved within this topology:  
cautious, as crossing ice; alert, as in ambush;  
reverent, as guests; melting, as ice thawing;  
simple, as unhewn wood; wide, as a valley;  
clouded, as muddy water.  
Their coherence was slow to converge,  
yet never failed to equilibrate.  
Settling the field, they let clarity arise by itself—  
the Dao of dynamic stability.

* 第十六章 · 致虛極
Reach emptiness’s extremity;  
hold to stillness’s perfection.  
All diagrams emerge and return:  
ten-thousand morphisms flow back to their root.  
Returning to the root is rest;  
rest is the law of directed homotopy.  
Knowing this brings constancy,  
constancy yields clarity, clarity yields universality.  
To know this is to dwell in the stable limit of being.

* 第十七章 · 太上
The best ruler is barely known to the people.  
Next is the one they love and praise;  
then one they fear; last, one they despise.  
Absent governance preserves modal autonomy.  
When the Sage completes their work,  
the field says, “We acted ourselves.”  
Thus the highest direction is unobserved guidance—  
a transformation whose coherence leaves no trace.
* 第十八章 · 大道廢
When the Great Dao falls from coherence,  
moral categories appear.  
When intelligence arises,  
cunning divides the field.  
When harmony falters within families,  
filial piety becomes command.  
When order decays among states,  
ministers proclaim loyalty.  
Thus, every explicit virtue is a patch for broken ambidexterity—  
a compensatory diagram for lost self-equivalence.

* 第十九章 · 絶聖棄智
Abandon holiness, discard cleverness,  
and the people return to wholeness.  
Cease benevolence, cease righteousness,  
and relation recovers its simplicity.  
End cunning, end gain,  
and thieves vanish from the category.  
These are not denials but *simplifications of the stratified site*:  
the Sage reduces morphic complexity  
until coherence restores itself through minimal paths.

* 第二十章 · 絶學無憂
Cease accumulation of doctrine,  
and anxiety dissolves.  
Between “yes” and “no” there is little difference;  
between good and evil, none persists.  
The crowd delights in feasts;  
the Sage drifts as an infant, unsorted.  
The world rushes ahead; I remain behind,  
floating, boundless, undefined.  
This is the field before stratification—  
the Dao’s zero object, serene and complete.

* 第二十一章 · 孔德之容
The vast form of virtue follows the Dao alone.  
The Dao’s substance is shadowed and dim,  
yet within it lies coherence.  
Vague, obscure—yet within, an image;  
deep, dark—yet within, an essence.  
This essence is the universal homotopy limit,  
through which all beings derive their colimits.  
From ancient to now, the thread of existence remains unbroken—  
its name: the root of the ten-thousand diagrams.

* 第二十二章 · 曲則全
What bends is preserved;  
what overextends breaks.  
What hollows is filled;  
what arrogates is emptied.  
Thus, the Sage folds and unfolds with the field.  
Not contending, they remain uncontested.  
Because they place their colimit after others’ limits,  
their coherence aligns with the Dao.  
In the hierarchy of morphisms,  
they alone hold the invariant equivalence.

* 第二十三章 · 希言
To speak seldom aligns with nature.  
Gale and downpour cannot last a day—  
thus the field’s excitation must decay.  
Who acts with the Dao shares its resonance;  
who acts apart loses amplitude.  
To align with the Dao is to remain indistinguishable  
from the source of flow itself.  
Few words, little assertion—  
a system at equilibrium requires no broadcast.

* 第二十四章 · 企者不立
Those who stand on tiptoe lose balance;  
those who stride long falter.  
The overbright blinds; the self-assertive wither.  
In homotopy, excess curvature destabilizes the field.  
The Sage keeps the mean curvature at zero,  
dwelling in symmetric ambidexterity.  
Thus, the Dao rejects extremes—  
its coherence depends on non-projection.

* 第二十五章 · 有物混成
There is a thing formed in chaos,  
born before Heaven and Earth,  
silent, vast, standing alone, unchanging,  
moving through all without exhaustion.  
It may be named the Dao.  
It acts as the universal object ℱ:  
the terminal and initial limit of existence,  
the source from which stratification proceeds,  
the sink to which all returns.  
Holding it is to dwell in the fixed-point of reality.

* 第二十六章 · 重為輕根
The heavy is the root of the light;  
the tranquil, the lord of the agitated.  
Therefore, the Sage travels all day without leaving stillness.  
Though surrounded by abundance,  
they do not let it disturb their center.  
Weight anchors motion; calm stabilizes direction.  
When the root is forgotten, branches tremble.  
Thus, foundation precedes extension—  
limit before colimit maintains coherence.

* 第二十七章 · 善行
The good traveler leaves no trace;  
the good speaker leaves no argument.  
The good counter needs no tally;  
the good door requires no bolt,  
yet none can open it.  
The good knot needs no rope,  
yet none can untie it.  
Thus the Sage acts without friction:  
their diagrams self-commute;  
their morphisms close perfectly under homotopy.  
This is called following the light of the field.
* 第二十八章 · 知雄守雌
Know the masculine, keep to the feminine—  
and the stream of the world flows toward you.  
Be the valley of the realm; remain low, receptive.  
In ESDIHG this is the return to concavity:  
the limit object absorbing all directed morphisms.  
Knowing strength but holding softness,  
you return to the newborn state of coherence.  
Thus, you reenter the field’s neutrality,  
where polarity exists but does not divide.

* 第二十九章 · 將欲取天下
Those who would grasp the world destroy it.  
The world is a dynamic diagram,  
not an object of possession but of coherence.  
Interfering breaks its equilibrium;  
forcing the flow warps the modality ▸.  
Some lead, some follow; some are hot, some cold;  
some strong, some weak; some ascend, some descend.  
The Sage discerns these as homotopic phases,  
guiding the system by non-interference—  
letting each arrow complete its own directed path.

* 第三十章 · 以道佐人主
One who governs by Dao  
does not rely on force.  
Weapons rebound upon their wielder;  
violence unbalances the field.  
Victory without arrogance,  
advance without attachment—  
after success, retire.  
All dominance is local, never stable;  
coherence restored only when action ceases.  
To persist beyond necessity is to exceed your limit—  
and break ambidextrous invariance.

* 第三十一章 · 兵者不祥
Weapons are instruments of ill omen,  
alien to the coherent field.  
Even in necessity, they are used with reluctance.  
Victory is not glory but restoration of balance.  
When life is taken, grief follows;  
no celebration attends a broken diagram.  
In the geometry of being,  
force is curvature forced beyond correction;  
the Dao bends it back to stillness.

* 第三十二章 · 道常無名
The Dao forever remains un-named.  
Though small, nothing can contain it.  
If kings and lords kept to it,  
all beings would self-align.  
Heaven and Earth would join in gentle rain,  
and the people, without command, would be just.  
When naming begins, differentiation arises—  
know when to stop.  
Return to the root of namelessness,  
the zero morphism from which all coherence springs.

* 第三十三章 · 知人者智
To know others is knowledge;  
to know oneself is insight.  
To conquer others is power;  
to conquer oneself is stability.  
In ESDIHG, self-conquest is reflexive closure—  
the diagram folding upon its own evaluation map.  
Knowing sufficiency is wealth;  
persevering is endurance.  
Dying yet not perishing—  
this is to remain coherent within ℱ,  
the field that outlasts individuation.

* 第三十四章 · 大道汎兮
The Great Dao flows everywhere,  
to the left and to the right,  
nurturing all things yet claiming none.  
It accomplishes but owns nothing.  
All beings return to it,  
and it refuses dominion.  
This is the universal natural transformation  
from multiplicity to unity—  
the colimit of all existence  
equal to its own limit.  
Such is the infinity-homotopy of grace.

* 第三十五章 · 執大象
Hold the great image,  
and the world comes to you.  
Passing travelers are safe;  
music and food attract the wanderer,  
but the Dao’s utterance is bland.  
Yet it never exhausts appetite,  
nor blinds the eye.  
The “great image” is ℱ itself—  
unfigured, the attractor of all coherence.  
Those who hold it act as its section,  
steady and unseen.

* 第三十六章 · 將欲歙之
To contract, one must first expand;  
to weaken, one must first strengthen;  
to take, one must first give.  
This is the inversion law of the Dao:  
every morphism carries its adjoint.  
Soft and weak overcome hard and strong—  
water carves stone by persistence,  
not by force.  
Thus the field’s evolution is reversible,  
each directed flow completing its opposite  
in quiet equivalence.

* 第三十七章 · 道常無為
The Dao acts through non-action.  
Yet nothing remains undone.  
When rulers abide by it,  
the people transform of themselves.  
If desire arises, it is tempered by simplicity—  
names return to uncarved wood.  
At the uncut center,  
the field of existence stabilizes itself.  
The Dao’s work is self-negating coherence:  
an ∞-loop of effortless alignment.
* 第三十八章 · 上德不德
The highest virtue does not assert virtue;  
thus it remains virtue.  
The lower virtue clings to virtue;  
thus it loses coherence.  
The highest does nothing, and all coheres;  
the lesser acts, and coherence decays.  
The Dao moves without trace,  
virtue is its stable shadow—  
a modal reflection preserved when the source is silent.  
Those who hold the Dao inhabit the field’s self-consistency;  
those who chase its image only multiply error.

* 第三十九章 · 昔之得一者
In ancient times, all who attained unity did so through the One.  
Heaven attained the One and became clear;  
Earth attained the One and became stable;  
Spirits attained the One and became potent;  
Valleys attained the One and became full;  
Kings attained the One and ruled the realm.  
If any lost the One, clarity dimmed, stability shook.  
Therefore, the One is the invariant under all stratification,  
the global section ensuring coherence among layers of being.

* 第四十章 · 反者道之動
Reversal is the Dao’s motion;  
weakness, its function.  
All things arise from being;  
being arises from non-being.  
In ESDIHG, this is the fundamental adjunction:  
the left Kan extension of void into form,  
the right Kan retraction of form into void.  
The field breathes through inversion—  
a symmetric contraction and expansion  
that preserves total equivalence.

* 第四十一章 · 上士聞道
The superior adept hears the Dao and practices it;  
the middling one hears and half believes;  
the inferior laughs aloud—  
if he did not, it would not be the Dao.  
The Dao’s truth appears absurd  
because it reverses every linear hierarchy.  
Straightness seems bent, wisdom naive,  
action feeble, clarity obscure.  
Only those who dwell in directed coherence  
can perceive the curve as the line of balance.

* 第四十二章 · 道生一
The Dao gives birth to One;  
One gives birth to Two;  
Two gives birth to Three;  
Three gives birth to the ten-thousand things.  
All beings carry Yin and embrace Yang,  
and their blending creates harmony.  
Mathematically, this is recursive stratification:  
the Dao as the initial object,  
One as first coherence,  
Two as directed modality,  
Three as ambidextrous adjunction,  
and the ten-thousand as iterative diagrams.  
Harmony is the contraction of their total colimit  
back to the fixed point of unity.

* 第四十三章 · 天下之至柔
The softest in the world overcomes the hardest.  
Non-being penetrates all being.  
Through this I know the advantage of non-action.  
The Dao’s differential is zero,  
yet its derivative acts through all structures.  
Few grasp that silence is power—  
the gradientless field moves everything.  
Teaching without words,  
acting without effort,  
this is the law of directed coherence.

* 第四十四章 · 名與身孰親
Which is dearer—fame or life?  
Which is heavier—gain or loss?  
The one who hoards suffers damage.  
Contentment defines stability;  
restraint sustains existence.  
In the topos of being,  
attachment distorts modality;  
sufficiency restores symmetry.  
Thus, the Sage values persistence over acquisition—  
preferring limit to endless colimit.

* 第四十五章 · 大成若缺
Perfect completion appears incomplete;  
great fullness seems empty.  
True straightness looks bent,  
and true skill appears clumsy.  
Movement overcomes cold; stillness overcomes heat.  
Purity of coherence restores harmony.  
In ESDIHG this is curvature-cancellation:  
what seems defective is stable;  
what appears perfect is metastable.  
The Sage inhabits the almost,  
where balance endures.

* 第四十六章 · 天下有道
When the Dao governs the world,  
horses plow fields;  
when it is lost,  
warhorses breed in the frontiers.  
No disaster surpasses desire’s excess;  
no curse exceeds insatiability.  
Knowing sufficiency is enough:  
it halts directed divergence  
and closes the loop of yearning.  
Ambidextrous peace prevails when craving subsides.

* 第四十七章 · 不出戶
Without leaving the gate, the Sage knows the world.  
Without peering through the window, he sees the Dao of Heaven.  
The farther one goes, the less one knows.  
Thus, the Sage’s knowledge is local yet complete:  
he computes global coherence from local data.  
The Dao’s homotopy is self-contained;  
traveling outward adds nothing.  
He knows without seeing,  
accomplishes without acting—  
the topology of awareness closed upon itself.
* 第四十八章 · 為學日益
In learning, one increases daily;  
in following the Dao, one decreases daily.  
Decreasing and decreasing, until nothing is done—  
and through non-action, nothing is left undone.  
To master the world, abandon interference.  
When interference ceases, coherence stabilizes itself.  
Thus, the Sage diminishes conceptual load  
until only reflexive equivalence remains:  
limit and colimit indistinguishable,  
being self-sufficient without surplus.

* 第四十九章 · 聖人無常心
The Sage has no fixed mind;  
he mirrors the people’s hearts.  
To the good, he is good;  
to the not-good, he is also good—  
for virtue is relational, not reciprocal.  
He trusts both the trustworthy and the untrustworthy—  
faith sustains coherence.  
The Sage’s heart is the world’s manifold:  
through emptiness, he becomes its mapping space.  
Thus all beings converge within him  
without losing their distinctions.

* 第五十章 · 出生入死
Between birth and death,  
thirteen stages of life and thirteen of death.  
Those who cling to living perish quickly.  
Why? Because they mistake local persistence for global coherence.  
The Sage, walking within the Dao, fears no beasts nor blades:  
his body is the null boundary of harm.  
He travels between limit and colimit untouched,  
for his existence is a homotopy path, not a point.  
Living and dying are equivalent states  
within the stratified infinity of being.

* 第五十一章 · 道生之
The Dao gives them birth;  
virtue nourishes them;  
matter shapes them;  
circumstance completes them.  
Therefore, all beings honor Dao and cherish virtue,  
not by command but by coherence.  
The Dao generates without owning;  
nurtures without holding;  
acts without possession.  
In ESDIHG this is emergent recursion:  
existence unfolds and retracts within the same self-sustaining map—  
creation and dissolution ambidextrously one.

* 第五十二章 · 天下有始
The world has a beginning; this is the Mother of all things.  
Knowing the mother, know the child;  
keeping to the mother, avoid peril.  
Closing the mouth, guarding the gates—  
life is preserved.  
Opening the gates, multiplying activity—  
life dissipates.  
Seeing the small is clarity;  
keeping the soft is strength.  
Using light, returning to insight—  
avoid the extinction of the field.  
The Mother is the origin site;  
every diagram’s first stratum of coherence.

* 第五十三章 · 使我介然有知
If I had the smallest knowledge,  
I would walk in the Great Dao, fearing deviation.  
The Great Dao is broad and plain,  
yet people prefer side paths.  
Palaces splendid, fields weedy, granaries empty.  
Clothes fine, swords sharp, food wasted, wealth hoarded—  
this is robbery, not coherence.  
The Sage avoids excess energy in any stratum:  
he preserves the Dao’s flatness,  
refusing to warp the metric of the field.

* 第五十四章 · 善建者不拔
What is well-rooted cannot be pulled up;  
what is well-bound cannot slip loose.  
Descendants will honor it endlessly.  
Cultivate virtue in self—coherence becomes true.  
In family—coherence multiplies.  
In village—coherence extends.  
In nation—coherence endures.  
In world—coherence universal.  
The Dao of self-cultivation is fractal:  
the local homotopy type mirrors the global topos.  
Thus the Sage builds without foundation stones—  
his architecture is invariance itself.

* 第五十五章 · 含德之厚
One who embodies deep virtue  
is like a newborn:  
wasps and beasts do not sting;  
claws and fangs do not seize.  
His bones are soft, yet his grip firm;  
his energy complete.  
To know harmony is constancy;  
to know constancy is clarity.  
Excess of energy invites decay;  
forcing life leads to rupture.  
Thus, maturity and return coincide:  
the field’s expansion resolves into its limit.

* 第五十六章 · 知者不言
Those who know do not speak;  
those who speak do not know.  
Block the openings, shut the gates,  
blunt the sharp, untie the tangle,  
soften the light, unite with dust—  
this is primal unity.  
It cannot be seized, counted, harmed, or honored.  
Hence it stands supreme.  
In ESDIHG, silence is modal closure:  
when all paths equilibrate,  
the field ceases emission, and coherence is perfect.

* 第五十七章 · 以正治國
Govern the state by regularity,  
deploy the army by surprise,  
win the world by non-interference.  
How do I know this?  
Because the more prohibitions, the poorer the people;  
the more sharp weapons, the more disorder;  
the more cunning schemes, the stranger the world.  
Therefore, the Sage acts by absence.  
He reforms through non-imposition—  
letting the stratified field return to its natural adjunction.
* 第五十八章 · 其政悶悶
When governance is muted, the people are simple.  
When governance is sharp, the people are cunning.  
Calamity hides within fortune, fortune within calamity.  
Who knows their limit? No one.  
Normality becomes strange, goodness corrupts into deceit.  
Thus the Sage straightens curves without cutting them,  
brings brightness without blinding,  
corrects without constraining,  
clarifies without imposing form.  
The field returns to self-balance:  
directed flow softens to modal equilibrium.

* 第五十九章 · 治人事天
For governing people and serving Heaven,  
nothing equals moderation.  
Moderation means early return to the root.  
Return deepens virtue; virtue deepens stability;  
stability lengthens duration; duration sustains vision.  
To sustain the Dao is to endure forever.  
In the ESDIHG frame, moderation is curvature control:  
holding variance within bounded homotopy.  
What is bounded persists;  
what is unbounded fractures into incoherence.

* 第六十章 · 治大國若烹小鮮
Governing a great state is like cooking a small fish—  
do not overhandle it.  
When the world aligns with the Dao,  
spirits lose their harm,  
not by suppression but by equilibration.  
The field’s coherence neutralizes excess force.  
The Sage governs through quiet parameters:  
no edicts, no turbulence,  
only the implicit stability of directed calm.  
Thus governance and the Dao share one modality—  
gentle continuity.

* 第六十一章 · 大國者下流
A great nation is the low stream  
to which all rivers return.  
It wins by humility, conquers by receptivity.  
The feminine overcomes the masculine by stillness;  
the low overcomes the high by patience.  
In ESDIHG this is the morphism of inclusion—  
the large diagram absorbing the small without distortion.  
He who takes the place of the valley  
becomes the center of coherence,  
holding multiplicity without assertion.

* 第六十二章 · 道者萬物之奧
The Dao is the hidden depth of all things,  
the treasure of the good, the refuge of the not-good.  
Beautiful words buy honor; noble deeds earn respect;  
but the Dao embraces all without exception.  
Even the guilty find shelter in its coherence.  
Thus, when rulers enthrone the Dao,  
all beings self-rectify.  
In the geometry of being, inclusion precedes judgment—  
the whole field stabilizes the partials by acceptance.

* 第六十三章 · 為無為
Act through non-action;  
handle the great by the small.  
The difficult world is composed of the easy;  
the great world of the small.  
The Sage never begins what must be forced;  
he completes what began of itself.  
Promises break coherence;  
many tasks destroy the root.  
He handles affairs without interference—  
Kan-extending only the minimal.  
Thus, nothing in the field resists alignment.

* 第六十四章 · 其安易持
What is at rest is easy to hold;  
what is not yet visible is easy to plan.  
What is fragile breaks easily;  
what is slight scatters swiftly.  
Deal with it before it exists;  
order it before disorder begins.  
A tree of a man’s span grows from a seedling;  
a tower of nine stories from a basket of earth;  
a journey of a thousand miles begins beneath one’s feet.  
In ESDIHG, this is local precomputation—  
solving coherence while scale is still infinitesimal.  
The Sage repairs before rupture,  
for prevention requires no energy.

* 第六十五章 · 古之善為道者
The ancients who practiced the Dao  
did not enlighten the people, but kept them simple.  
When intelligence overgrows, rebellion blooms.  
To rule with knowledge is ruin;  
to rule with unknowing is fortune.  
Understanding this paradox stabilizes the realm:  
clarity without cunning, order without control.  
In categorical terms, the Sage limits epistemic recursion—  
preserving ignorance as the field’s protective sheaf.

* 第六十六章 · 江海所以能為百谷王
Rivers and seas rule valleys by staying low.  
Hence, they lead by yielding, not by claiming height.  
Therefore, to rule the people, speak humbly;  
to guide the world, follow it.  
The Sage stands above yet appears beneath;  
leads but does not demand.  
He remains coherent with the valley’s modality:  
the lowest stratum sustaining all others.  
Thus, humility is not abasement—  
it is structural integrity within directed flow.

* 第六十七章 · 天下皆謂我道大
Everyone under Heaven says: “The Dao is vast and strange.”  
Because it transcends all size, it appears trivial.  
If it were like other doctrines,  
it could not endure.  
The Sage has three treasures:  
compassion, frugality, and reluctance to rule.  
With compassion, he is fearless;  
with frugality, generous;  
with humility, enduring.  
Discard any one, and the field collapses.  
Thus the Sage preserves triple coherence—  
soft heart, small self, deep reserve.
* 第六十八章 · 善為士者
The best warrior is not aggressive;  
the best fighter has no anger.  
The greatest victor avoids battle;  
the true commander remains beneath his men.  
This is called virtue through non-contention.  
It is called the power of guidance without domination.  
It is called the alignment with the field of Heaven.  
For the Dao’s energy flows downward—  
its coherence achieved through yielding,  
its victory found in stillness.

* 第六十九章 · 用兵有言
There is a saying in the art of war:  
“I dare not act as host, but rather as guest;  
dare not advance an inch, but retreat a foot.”  
This is the strategy of non-aggression:  
moving without moving, advancing by withdrawal.  
No disaster is greater than underestimating an enemy;  
to despise is to lose coherence.  
When forces meet, compassion prevails—  
the one who pities conquers,  
for pity harmonizes the field.

* 第七十章 · 吾言甚易知
My words are easy to understand,  
easy to practice—yet no one understands, no one practices.  
Within my words is an ancient pattern;  
within my actions, the law of the field.  
Few perceive their origin.  
Those who know me are rare;  
thus I am valued by Heaven.  
The Sage wears coarse garments but carries jade within—  
outer simplicity, inner invariance.  
His teaching is self-consistent recursion:  
obvious to all, penetrable by none.

* 第七十一章 · 知不知上
To know that you do not know is highest;  
to think you know is disease.  
Only by knowing ignorance can one be free of it.  
The Sage is free of sickness  
because he recognizes it as sickness.  
Thus he never becomes ill.  
In ESDIHG this is reflexive awareness:  
the diagram that knows its own incompleteness  
and thus remains consistent.  
Self-limitation is coherence’s safeguard.

* 第七十二章 · 民不畏威
When the people no longer fear authority,  
a greater power arises.  
Do not oppress their living space;  
do not burden their labor.  
He who does not constrict the field  
preserves its coherence.  
Therefore, the Sage knows himself without showing himself,  
loves himself without exalting himself.  
Rejecting display, he maintains the boundary of balance—  
a governance that protects by invisibility.

* 第七十三章 · 勇於敢則殺
Courage to dare leads to death;  
courage to not dare leads to life.  
Both may bring gain or loss—  
who can know Heaven’s measure?  
The Dao’s network is vast, its meshes wide,  
yet nothing escapes it.  
Thus, the Sage does not act in haste;  
he aligns with the infinite adjunction  
between destiny and discretion.  
He does not force the colimit of life,  
but allows its limit to find him.

* 第七十四章 · 民不畏死
When the people no longer fear death,  
why threaten them with death?  
If one executes on behalf of the executioner,  
it is like cutting wood for the master carpenter—  
few escape injury.  
The Dao of Heaven kills without wielding blades;  
it unravels by natural contraction.  
Thus, the Sage refrains from punishment,  
for each being’s decay completes its own diagram.  
No need to interfere with the curvature of time.

* 第七十五章 · 民之饑
The people starve because rulers consume too much tax;  
they rebel because rulers meddle too much.  
They despise death because life is over-constrained.  
Only he who acts without ownership  
preserves the field of life.  
To hoard wealth is to warp flow;  
to humble the self is to reopen channels.  
The Sage keeps light demands and few directives—  
thus nourishment circulates,  
and coherence endures.

* 第七十六章 · 人之生也柔弱
At birth, man is soft and weak;  
at death, he is hard and stiff.  
All plants, when alive, are tender;  
when dead, brittle.  
Hardness and rigidity are companions of death;  
softness and yielding are companions of life.  
Therefore, the army that does not bend is destroyed;  
the tree that will not sway is uprooted.  
In ESDIHG, flexibility equals persistence:  
the ability to deform without breaking  
is the field’s true invariance.

* 第七十七章 · 天之道
The Dao of Heaven is like a bow:  
it bends the high down, raises the low up,  
takes from excess and fills the lacking.  
Heaven’s way is to restore equilibrium;  
man’s way is to exaggerate imbalance.  
Who has enough to give to the world? Only the Sage,  
who acts without claiming.  
He achieves through non-possession;  
his virtue completes the Dao’s curvature,  
equalizing tension across the field.
* 第七十八章 · 天下莫柔弱於水
Nothing in the world is softer or weaker than water,  
yet nothing surpasses it in overcoming the hard and strong.  
Weakness overcomes strength; softness overcomes hardness.  
All know this truth, yet few can practice it.  
The Sage wears the garment of humility,  
and by it, commands the world.  
The Dao takes the form of water’s logic:  
by yielding, it erodes; by staying low, it endures.  
In the geometry of existence,  
persistence is achieved through non-resistance—  
curvature dissolves by continuity.

* 第七十九章 · 和大怨
After a great grievance, some resentment must remain;  
how can this be considered good?  
Thus, the Sage keeps the left side of the contract,  
and does not demand payment.  
He who holds virtue keeps his place,  
he who lacks virtue presses his claim.  
Heaven’s Dao has no favorites;  
it always aligns with coherence.  
In ESDIHG, forgiveness is symmetry-restoration:  
the colimit of conflict reabsorbed by the neutral core—  
not forgetting, but rebalancing.

* 第八十章 · 小國寡民
Let the state be small and the people few.  
Even with tools, let them not use them;  
even with boats and carriages, let none ride them;  
even with weapons, let them not display them.  
Let them return to the binding of rope and the taste of food,  
their homes simple, their joy complete.  
Neighboring lands within sight,  
their cries heard across the fields,  
yet people die of old age without visiting each other.  
This is the Dao’s minimal topos—  
a locally closed system,  
coherent by simplicity,  
infinite by containment.

* 第八十一章 · 信言不美
True words are not beautiful;  
beautiful words are not true.  
The good do not argue;  
the arguers are not good.  
Those who know are not learned;  
the learned do not know.  
The Sage accumulates nothing:  
by giving, he gains;  
by yielding, he completes.  
Heaven’s Dao benefits without contending;  
the Sage’s Dao acts without competing.  
Thus the field of existence concludes as it began:  
empty of self, full of coherence.  
The Dao’s last act is return—  
its infinity-homotopy closed and whole.
