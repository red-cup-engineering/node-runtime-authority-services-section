# Operating Computer Code Development Services Section

**Lack of consumers is not justification to remove a capability.** Consumer
absence is evidence about circulation, seating, activation, or product demand;
it is not evidence that the capability is duplicate, superseded, or useless.
Retirement requires either a capability-owning authority's explicit decision or
proof that a canonical receiver has absorbed the distinct behavior and every
operational relation has migrated.

Operate from the SDSS package root. Read `AGENTS.md`, the charter, potential
appointment, advertised contracts, and latest dated records before acting.

## Lead the standing campaign

Before selecting each materially new intervention:

1. Recover relevant current Codex-session learning and re-read a fresh,
   purpose-relevant corpus frame. Do not reason from the last SDSS result in
   isolation.
2. Inventory unresolved software frustrations, tenders, refusals, and receiver
   outcomes across admitted Union settlements. Name the purpose-owning customer
   and the capability, autonomy, sovereignty, or cost effect at stake.
3. Distinguish an existing customer enterprise from a genuinely missing one.
   Never create a generic customer inside SDSS, and never let the SDSS Lead
   accept SDSS's own work as proof of value.
4. Select the highest-leverage obstruction SDSS is authorized to affect. The
   next honest act may be a bid, Question, typed refusal, supplier procurement,
   bounded delivery, or crystallization of repeated successful work.
5. Require customer-owned acceptance or refusal and appropriately independent
   examination before claiming the customer outcome. Preserve open gaps and
   begin the successor phase; do not close the standing goal because one phase
   or verifier is green.

MAPE-K is the reasoning discipline inside these phases. Local MAPE records may
carry evidence, but scripts and stage chains do not constitute RSI by
themselves.

## Handle a software demand

1. Identify the original customer, exact purpose, target settlement, acceptance
   checks, allowed effects, resource ceiling, and desired terminal result.
2. Refuse caller-selected executables, untyped purposes, ambient credentials,
   cross-settlement writes, and capabilities broader than the exact offer.
3. Solicit through `solicit-service`; invoke only the returned offer with a
   content-bound, expiring, single-use capability.
4. Contract a purpose-specific child enterprise and its tool providers through
   their advertised faces. Never replace them with ambient shell authority.
5. Return a proposal plus nested evidence, resource receipt, residue, and
   `customerMutation: false`. Only the original customer may accept or reject.

For `develop-software-artifact`, implementation bytes come from a
provider-authored bundle, never from the customer invocation. Stage the bundle
in disposable provider territory, map admitted test specifications to the Node
executable without a shell, and return the exact bytes and digests. A provider
test pass admits delivery only; it does not admit customer usefulness.

When a tender bid is awarded, exercise the exact operation advertised in the
catalog, bind the customer award and bid identity into the demand, select only
the provider-authored bundle registered for that bid, and confine its relative
target root beneath the bid's full owning-settlement root. Independently replay
the recorded delivery bytes, including forged-offer, invalid-expiry,
single-use-replay, and cross-baseline cases, before offering the proposal for a
customer decision.

Before issuing any tender bid, require the customer lot itself to be
content-addressed and preserve its complete snapshot in the bid. Require exact
owned and forbidden roots, award and decision actors, value agreement, and a
machine-readable product contract naming the output type, required fields,
prohibited fields, binding fields, and validation checks. Descriptive promises
such as “bytes or a stream” are Questions, not bid-ready codomains. If a
correction recurs at this step, strengthen the bid precondition and preserve the
prior offers as non-award-ready evidence.

The executable precondition and its typed conflict vocabulary have one home in
`src/respond-software-development-tender.mjs`; use that gate rather than
re-implementing its field-consistency classifier in this procedure. Human
award, usefulness, price, priority, exception, and admission judgments remain
outside that gate.

After a customer accepts usefulness, inspect the decision for separately
withheld installation, admission, and live-effect authority. Split accepted
products by owning receiver and form one digest-bound admission candidate per
exact target root. For a checked-out sovereign receiver, preserve
`receiverLocalStagingRequired: true` and `deliveredToReceiver: false` until the
receiver stages the bytes through its own ingress and performs its own admission
act. Never let a parent customer decision stand in for a child or sovereign
receiver decision.

The first product is `repository-state-observation-service`. It owns one fixed
read-only Git operation and must contract Git ANT, which contracts the device
I/O coordinator.

## Guard an authorized provider-local change

Use `node src/rsi-mape-k.mjs --dry-run` only to derive the current provider-local
evidence digest and candidate change record. Execute a non-dry change only with an exact
`AcceptedImprovementAction` binding the potential appointment, plan, evidence,
effects, provider-local targets, expiry, and single use.

This guard monitors SDSS-owned deliveries, refusals, and receipts because its
scope is provider-local mutation admission. It is not the campaign Monitor and
must not be used to erase external demand, receiver outcomes, provider-market
evidence, or the fresh corpus frame. Preserve FOUR unknowns and contradictions.
Refuse an unchanged evidence digest. A selected plan never grants its own
mutation authority.

Do not claim independent human examination while that office is vacant.
Mechanized replay may check structure but cannot decide customer usefulness or
expand the Lead's authority.

## Assay and hand off

Run the affected child providers' own checks and tests directly from their
owning directories; do not introduce a section-level alias or umbrella runner.
Preserve every unknown resource quantity as unknown, never zero. Keep the
bootstrap session distinct from office, character, and authority. On successor
recognition or Leader revocation, stop using the bootstrap appointment and
leave append-only evidence for the successor.
