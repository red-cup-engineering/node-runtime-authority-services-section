*/Félix Guattari/* ([[id:e2aec9fe-e525-4452-b96b-e6a47508671a][1930]]-[[id:a74890ec-b3a7-44e1-af1c-04e9fedfb4f7][1992]]) was a [[id:a5072f0c-5cf1-4d0c-b6d9-fd167ea258b2][French]] [[id:471f7f8f-73a6-41f6-af44-8db0a7ff270e][psychoanalyst]], [[id:02f90700-7b36-450c-9b36-4ec013faa94b][philospher]], and [[id:cd489fa6-d90e-49fc-b977-6e47fadd6e26][political activist]]. He is best known for [[id:3512096b-1dd2-4386-b9bb-a185f82f5e4c][collaborating]] with [[id:2b216276-426a-4939-909f-42cf78b9e8b3][Gilles Deleuze]] to [[id:f7cc4bac-8a9d-43a2-a76f-4e014ae70405][author]] [[id:d1f72649-2cef-46a5-820a-e287f6847956][Anti-Oedipus]] ([[id:b6dacaae-629a-40e6-93d5-76f70a6f4ae7][1972]]) and [[id:8f9957f7-1cd8-4426-bbe1-a81c1aae8894][A Thousand Plateaus]] ([[id:b48656ef-a88f-4d89-95c7-5961b73a3d1e][1980]]), which form the two volumes of [[id:ceb3806d-86b8-453c-b469-82f524992817][Capitalism and Schizophrenia]]. These texts challenged traditional [[id:33310308-574a-4442-accb-a4503e0add74][psychoanalysis]], [[id:61f7f4a0-8e58-4f1a-9114-30e296301176][Marxism]], and [[id:780ec278-03e8-4460-b5d4-a12d3ce51c00][structuralism]], introducing concepts like [[id:d588916c-e345-4c84-bc2e-73c80a1ee013][schizoanalysis]], [[id:0357d901-5cb3-4633-9dc4-ce1526d6b064][deterritorialization]], and [[id:0ac8112c-4b6c-4d8a-bd69-2c697665b089][assemblage theory]].

*Félix Guattari* [[id:a1a802c7-32b8-4d24-8f9b-c3192d30bbf4][trained]] under [[id:6c191fb5-2aa0-4d8b-beba-a7ea32c7f9f5][Jacques Lacan]] and worked at [[id:159b1b85-a6b0-4f08-8fa4-f67d80955857][La Borde psychiatric clinic]].
