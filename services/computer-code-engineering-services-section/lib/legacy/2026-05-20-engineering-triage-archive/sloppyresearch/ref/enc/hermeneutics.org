#+title: hermeneutics
#+date: [2025-08-27 Wed 07:51]
#+index: hermeneutics

*/Hermeneutics/* is the [[id:fb63f9e1-b14d-4745-bda3-b018ecd864d5][theory]] and [[id:bf97ea35-5e01-4ffb-8137-fb1621c64815][methodology]] of [[id:5238f373-145c-46ab-a35f-d64c80a127a5][interpreting]] [[id:a547e3dc-3cc7-4ddb-9fe4-7dfe3f96dd70][meaning]].

Originally, *hermeneutics* was limited to the theory and methodology of [[id:e453e190-ddbe-4ba1-89e8-e7511e4467d8][exegesis]], or interpretation of [[id:ad9b3afb-460c-4664-8a04-1f6d93ef5cd9][scripture]], but contemporarily may include all [[id:09fd88b6-87be-4af3-835f-daf4514553ff][communication]] and [[id:3a0f6593-ac75-4a3f-9407-afb1bb2fa27d][knowledge]].
