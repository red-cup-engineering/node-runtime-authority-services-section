#+title: Grimm's Law
#+date: [2025-08-25 Mon 08:48]

*/Grimm's Law/*, also known as the First Germanic Consonant Shift, is a liguistic principle that describes the phonological changes that transformed [[id:415d924d-3c5c-4c3d-99cf-4c3bf5c154d4][Proto-Indo-European language]] into [[id:5248a5a2-7e33-47c2-96c0-51874c92ccd4][Proto-Germanic language]].

First [[id:dc21a3aa-a339-4106-8a19-cbaba0a0ded4][described]] by [[id:92180e3d-47e3-4c68-88bd-f762183cebf3][Rasmus Rask]] and later formalized by [[id:8ed71703-e1d6-40fe-8f73-0d8481a0945a][Jacob Grimm]]. [cite:@Grimm1822], Grimm's Law introduced the concept that pjhonological changes are regular and predictable.
