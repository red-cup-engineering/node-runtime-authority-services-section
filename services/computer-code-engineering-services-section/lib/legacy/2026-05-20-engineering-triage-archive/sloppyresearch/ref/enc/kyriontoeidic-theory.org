#+title: kyriontoeidic theory
#+date: [2025-09-13 Sat 21:54]
#+index: kyriontoeidics

*Kyriontoeidic theory/* is the [[id:fb63f9e1-b14d-4745-bda3-b018ecd864d5][theory]] of [[id:e6e23b56-f59f-4218-8356-cc1d1d3a3734][kyriontoeidic analysis]].

It's [[id:b5c529e9-5ccc-444b-b5b2-1164570c847e][theoretical lineage]] is in [[id:4dfb51d4-1ca0-4305-aee8-b71f6d72c896][post-structuralist theory]] and [[id:b68b42cc-c4c8-41c8-8e03-c1aecf071752][genre theory]] (among others, probably).

It was [[id:fe5f19f5-4966-42c0-bc11-f5b5f7031238][created]] by [[id:40cbaad9-af3c-4573-b356-ae116687e483][emsenn]] as a way of discussing the [[id:3ecf31e2-67b6-46c4-923d-2e412aa7d908][commodity]] that appears to be produced by certain [[id:f09d5f1e-1e92-4111-8fc2-833b553bae8a][capitalist labor processes]] related to [[id:aed0d126-20ad-4104-9d4e-d41fed7fe25b][neurotic liberalism]], [[id:338c7b6b-8f83-46fb-8323-41800a502684][magical nominalism]], and [[id:7ae00144-f028-4b25-a533-c64d081de2bd][californication]].

It's a part of my broader thinking on how [[id:ca7fc9c5-9820-4bf2-ba1f-6b90f323d7f4][formalism]] has shaped the world:

A [[id:c6dacfca-258c-442c-beba-0b0cf02ce25d][kyriontoeidon]] forms when [[id:7fa81316-d308-412e-a551-0203653d3959][anticipation]] ([[id:0c45a2b5-f4fd-4a08-b99e-5b617ec7378e][incited]] by [[id:d99897f9-50de-48de-ad3d-e6ee7c02ba5a][linear temporality]]) modifies an [[id:46f1f123-cd69-4562-b13c-ca0445f90f5e][ontoeidon]], an [[id:b6681877-e441-49cc-af6c-14bd0cf66876][eidon]] formed by [[id:7ce83293-bb4b-4787-953b-5d8b7506cc78][ontologizing]].

I'm having a hard time describing it, but it's something to do with how the idea of things should be a certain way comes to be, because linear time gives us expectations, proto-possession, etc.
