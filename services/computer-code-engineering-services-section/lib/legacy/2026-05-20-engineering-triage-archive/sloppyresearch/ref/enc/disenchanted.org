#+index: magic!disenchanted

*/Disenchanted/* is the condition of being [[id:518bb07b-ae7e-4a3c-8c3c-e895f3679dfc][not]] [[id:3ab2d66c-b65d-42c2-8df6-ca57d22d9d83][enchanted]].

Depending on use, it might relate to [[id:5ab08606-75b1-4a88-9dbe-da5930e23558][Max Weber]]'s idea of [[id:8e80c203-cb8d-4ff4-9b3f-e4aba85e20d9][Entzauberung]], or disenchantment, who borrowed the term from [[id:28161ac9-79ed-4c33-8439-8e9e59ed62c2][Friedrich Schiller]], to describe the cultural rationalization and devaluation of religion in modernized, bureaucratic, industrial, secular, Western society.

So, to be disenchanted is to specifically have had some [[id:0c16d724-7466-46fd-80d5-e02a6ac07fe9][magic]] or [[id:83838eb1-61a5-48bd-960a-5be8b5528c92][enchantment]] removed through an [[id:32e00173-c695-4bb6-b0d2-9dc17fec128b][act]] or [[id:4a53867b-afa3-437b-b979-385597e0e616][process]], not just not being enchanted, which is what I would call [[id:1c700f9d-3202-45ef-a414-b58170493a66][mundane]].
