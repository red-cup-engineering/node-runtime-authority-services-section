*/Conceptual analysis/* is the [[id:ae374582-4b8f-4caa-b6d5-a0e747d1a204][philosophical analysis]] of a [[id:fd6ecbed-9bdf-4570-b4b2-5ff7744c0a96][concept]]; finding the [[id:1a4d1413-cd45-4a1c-93ab-3663d2058fbe][description]] or [[id:e5713ab3-f9b9-45da-99bb-8a9b67891ca6][definition]] that is [[id:34355157-6f8f-4a47-9286-bb5dfdec44e0][true]] for that concept: what is the [[id:a547e3dc-3cc7-4ddb-9fe4-7dfe3f96dd70][meaning]] that is [[id:fde2d52c-548e-4520-9e82-b29206b37ade][referenced]] when a concept is reference?

Conceptual analysis has [[id:17818943-5372-4867-965c-3983ba0b641e][utility]] because [[id:f427ec74-c16a-4950-887e-0b40dc5cee0d][philosophy]] often deals with [[id:dccd106a-5450-4e41-863a-d02b17fd9468][abstractions]] and [[id:edd38d25-a123-49e7-ba79-7b491260632f][ideas]]: [[id:1f810c64-c53a-4e7e-8d9a-1a93dfbb1b7a][structures]] that aren't necessarily [[id:450e2fc1-42e2-4f2e-8384-cdd2f091183e][coherent]] or [[id:040274d7-0a80-4da3-97b0-19bdc481672f][legible]] without [[id:dc21a3aa-a339-4106-8a19-cbaba0a0ded4][describing]] them.

Conceptual analysis essentially involves [[id:8ecc9186-df5a-4e19-b352-57f362f9a4b2][examining]] a concept's [[id:40a4244e-a940-40f0-a4bb-0e6e265e96f3][relationships]] to other concepts. There are different [[id:7d544b44-cfed-4831-b561-31792e9c8e07][modes of conceptual analysis]]:

- [[id:ca986d6f-a31d-4e5b-9dda-5474624588c4][two-dimensionalist conceptual analysis]]
