#+title: multi-user dungeon
#+date: [2025-08-23 Sat 10:32]
#+index: multi-user dungeon

A */multi-user dungeon/* (MUD, also defined as muti-user dimension or domain) is a [[id:d6db98dc-1126-40e7-8ddc-8b3897be5c0c][virtual environment]] where multiple [[id:671fcda5-e8e7-451e-a6d5-d3131f39f89f][users]] [[id:b80125d4-20bf-4c62-9cc3-de19fdfa6f73][interact]] using a [[id:6db3533f-dabe-4555-8179-e4ba18ba4acc][command line interface]]. MUDs are often [[id:4ebd1e19-d32b-4c5e-aa69-447ec3983a7a][role-playing games]], so may be [[id:dc21a3aa-a339-4106-8a19-cbaba0a0ded4][described]] as [[id:076e6740-d67c-4f28-9f24-d5efe7c79073][similar]] to [[id:acb6d70b-42b9-4e13-bc91-468f2a62ab1c][interactive fiction]], [[id:893f3eeb-a075-4cc6-991b-531be087d8a6][tabletop role-playing games]], and [[id:250f3db1-fa3e-4724-ad1c-51141619e2a3][online chat rooms]].

MUDs are a [[id:5b2829a0-28f0-41ee-8334-b78aa62ab5cc][predecessor]] to [[id:446eeda2-1110-40c8-bcce-eaf49d1e5003][massively-multiplayer online role-playing games]] (MMORPGs).

* History
** Prehistory
- [[id:46c96c2d-92b5-4ff9-a10d-e19afb18fb8b][1975ce]], [[id:3e52ce0f-a250-4744-9a1b-a1738f523510][Colossal Cave Adventure]] by [[id:5a9b0814-7906-47c1-9779-c720b15ba3e1][Will Crowther]]
  - introduced [[id:936437db-55a5-4e33-9e9a-4893ff1f9ea8][text-interface virtual environment]]
  - [[id:904e243d-d2f6-49a8-8b0a-0ee3aa8b6199][Don Woods]] brought in interactive fiction
- [[id:1c3e64aa-a2cf-43e3-9f6b-2ccd33184919][Zork]]
- [[id:133d8bcc-c37e-4dba-9f14-a76fe4196722][ARPANET]]
** Origins
- late [[id:7655ce6a-a74e-45b9-ae3b-12e6149b8d2c][1978ce]], [[id:4eefcc80-e16b-470d-94dc-54e2243d4cea][Roy Trubshaw]], student at [[id:c961a67c-89d9-4348-b5db-e180b10332a8][University of Essex]]
  - began writing a software literally called MUD
  - on a [[id:dd4db8b6-eb96-4179-9471-55a19232c378][DEC PDP-10]] using [[id:73b215bc-b09d-4b76-935a-5e93bb52e9e2][MACRO-10]]
  - rewrote in in [[id:43fb3490-e396-45da-9195-0a406dead36a][BCPL]], precursor to [[id:e5388e06-d87e-4cff-8910-ef101a96108b][C programming language]]
  - named MUD for /Dungeon/ a [[id:455749ef-89f2-4154-a618-bb5377df7793][FORTRAN]] fork of Zork
    - thus, not a genre name, but literally just, multi-user /Dungeon/
- In 1979, MUD1 was rewritten in MUDDL, MUD Definition Language, by Trubshaw and Bartle
- in [[id:b48656ef-a88f-4d89-95c7-5961b73a3d1e][1980]], Trubshaw graduated and handed project to [[id:ab4c9ef6-69c3-43f8-bebd-8d0e6c635752][Richard Bartle]]
  - notable, 1980 is when Essex connected to ARPANET
- 1983: 2am to 7am, British Telecom's Packet Switch Stream allowed access to the PDP-10
- By 1984, MUD1 was hosted on the PDP-20 at Dundee College of Technology
  - also in 84, [[id:498510e6-6ba6-4034-9f26-fcdde1942c45][CompuNet]] licensed MUD1 for it's Commodore 64 network, where it ran until 1987
- 1987, [[id:30bfb96e-1ff1-4003-b04e-e0747c683d57][CompuServe]] relicensed under the name British Legends, and pressured Bartle to shut down the Essex instance of MUD1, which was done in October 1987
- [[id:0b6feb88-ff97-4708-b98a-0f9426838f8b][1989ce]], [[id:ccfdafd6-4b4f-4640-8c5b-b228091f77d9][Lars Pensjö C]], developed by [[id:32577b02-46f7-42a8-b609-599006bcfabb][Lars Pensjö]], [[id:b7014013-8f96-463c-b45f-c689fe90f20a][University of Gothenburg]]
  - [[id:b1225383-d196-40cf-a4ed-c8838804e1d0][object-oriented programming language]] for building [[id:46dab219-c7a1-4d18-8152-aeae6a92906a][LPMUD]]
  - introduced [[id:de5cda39-6fa8-4f17-a11e-3c6aca2b66e8][MUD engine]] and [[id:24ee4b0f-87d5-4fc6-8d3d-a2972410e1fa][MUD library]] [[id:c9cc4a84-0f4a-4bd6-8ebe-5048131187bc][bifurcation]].
- [[id:1c8ba075-a814-4111-9ee1-46fc2383a56e][1990-03-27]], [[id:eaf7399b-dff0-4ead-9529-61d78177b699][Hans Henrik Stærfeldt]] announced [[id:05cd536a-39e6-4e64-b386-1c2833e612e6][DikuMUD]] (DIKU = Datalogisk Institut Kobenhavns Universitet ([[id:338bd4ab-90a3-44ed-9099-c078bfd2399f][University of Copenhagen]]))
- [[id:556807e3-6061-4047-8460-f2106d87002b][1991-02-03]], first public DikuMUD instance
