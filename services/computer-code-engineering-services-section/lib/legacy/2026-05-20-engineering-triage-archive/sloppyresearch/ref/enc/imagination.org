*/Imagination/* has been described as a [[id:a71bc266-d531-457c-bd96-185ae0e9a267][mode of intuition]]. ([[id:ef0b3c4b-006f-4078-90a0-210f2bea2c0c][Kant]]) [[id:91257384-4e2b-4425-987b-89d4df1592f1][Imagining]] is [[id:7286f2fe-ff66-4b63-ad16-2c0955d0cc48][interfacing]] with imagination.

* Modes of Imagination

Imagination has been described as having its own [[id:61cca185-135c-4b4a-8182-9e021886a700][modes of imagination]].

- [[id:cbe70035-5fba-4f01-9775-c4f4d9c1ddbd][Reproductive imagination]] is [[id:ef3433a0-d9d2-453b-82de-f5b2afe9bfa6][remembering]] and [[id:84b66210-7993-4545-b647-149cc3b99eda][modifying]] [[id:5442ac13-aa44-45b4-8288-52a8602c31cf][experience]].
- [[id:587e0fb6-2875-441a-8848-b07c18586dd7][Productive imagination]] is [[id:fe5f19f5-4966-42c0-bc11-f5b5f7031238][creating]] [[id:5442ac13-aa44-45b4-8288-52a8602c31cf][experience]]. (Kant)

* Differentiating Imagination

Imagination has been [[id:037f0366-63ec-4c09-92fc-9c3300086c59][differentiated]] from [[id:095a85b7-646a-4860-9c0e-320bf2cf6619][perception]] for [[id:d2dd4008-f6be-48cc-a53e-cd64854f2afc][including]] [[id:f571bfdc-5f16-47eb-8d4d-9e8e2392cfa3][intention]]. ([[id:56f14eae-064b-45b2-880a-8d949e2e392b][Sartre]])

Imagination may focus on imagining that which is [[id:19d25f43-167a-4cfc-9d18-aea0736cf637][unreal]]. ([[id:6d9aa7c9-f557-4778-9f72-f90b3e4714e2][James Rowland Angell]])

Imagination may be a [[id:09e9ca8a-d4dd-4948-b020-446bdc3138b3][mode of making meaning]].

* Science

Imagining physical experience has been demonstrated to create similar neural activations to the real experience.
