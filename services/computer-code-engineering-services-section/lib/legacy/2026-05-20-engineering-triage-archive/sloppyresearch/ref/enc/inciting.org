*/inciting/* is an [[id:32e00173-c695-4bb6-b0d2-9dc17fec128b][act]] where a [[id:8b78e297-2d6b-452a-bbea-c53bd2cf35ed][complex system]] causes a [[id:4e67acb5-1448-44ea-99a0-05cb1f3014ad][subsystem]] to act.

I use the term in a way influenced by [[id:e7d11cd2-1c74-406e-960e-0d56f90e5331][Michel Foucault]]'s [[id:e5fb6e7d-b9d2-419a-94ee-43626e138a6e][l'incitation]], but to refer to the more general [[id:c289a415-7cf4-44fd-a5dd-0139c6c56f0e][relational dynamic]] represented.
