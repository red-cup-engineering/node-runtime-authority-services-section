*/Ontogenrism/* is an [[id:9e40095c-cc98-4eaf-96a6-5e964a3750df][ontological process]] in which [[id:7ce83293-bb4b-4787-953b-5d8b7506cc78][ontologizing]] is [[id:ccfa42b1-16cc-448e-9f09-5577b91b6464][genring]]. [[id:6f2fa75a-9118-470d-8d4c-fc6bcef30c55][Coined]] by [[id:40cbaad9-af3c-4573-b356-ae116687e483][emsenn]], /ontogenrism/ describes the [[id:4611cff9-6da3-43a1-bdb8-faaaae75c8ac][contemporary global kyriarchy]]'s [[id:8ac92a69-9252-44d7-8a4c-dbaec65c0a0c][dominant]] [[id:11f1242d-1f2a-4e0d-bd55-7c80aab098ae][ontological mode]], which may be an [[id:a2f8cccb-a88a-43fe-b24c-2fba05dfb78f][elementary process]] of [[id:5a898ac0-0046-4074-b0d5-ed6b60aac677][liberalism]], as a [[id:bbf97e7c-41b5-4fd8-83a6-b29820f7529b][recursive process]] by which [[id:3d5c27c4-d1b8-434f-b821-4fc534bee37f][kyriarchist systems]] [[id:f194f45f-6211-4f39-aa3f-a7eaa39dd616][format]] [[id:00298e02-93cb-41cc-a77f-ee90289e0139][existence]] into [[id:8a6e7736-caa0-4b01-a53e-f401509e0022][legible]], [[id:5fc06c32-7f4c-41c5-9f79-dc9b6d5a4a25][governable]] [[id:4695cc5c-70e9-442f-8a89-4b14fff5be3e][subjects]] through a [[id:e5987b3f-f07f-47a5-ae7e-d16736b5a04d][narrative]] of [[id:7fa81316-d308-412e-a551-0203653d3959][anticipation]] and [[id:bc49235c-12a6-4a0e-9cc9-b74cc00ae84c][entitlement]] [[id:65f6d703-8d44-433d-abc7-c8a93578309b][affirmed]] by its [[id:e3f39153-5c1f-4999-ba28-34407c15ba64][a priori determination]].

Contrasted with ontological processes outside the [[id:54c64b48-3b50-443c-973b-7b620073905e][lineage of contemporary kyriarchist ontologizing]], /ontogenrism/ is [[id:037f0366-63ec-4c09-92fc-9c3300086c59][differentiated]] by what it must [[id:4549d026-36be-4ef4-8f43-4d6ea04c9b44][assume]] to be [[id:34355157-6f8f-4a47-9286-bb5dfdec44e0][true]]: existence is [[id:f91410bc-dcdc-4406-91fa-ba37e8b88bf4][immanently]] [[id:e0d9fe99-251a-42a3-bf56-b13085ab691d][coherent]].

This simple [[id:4dcf4f23-40af-43a4-9ef0-8711ae95518d][assumption]] has [[id:bc03e36a-a423-4e79-a9ec-f6909b7df428][developed]] alongside the contemporary global kyriarchy from [[id:5d60878e-172c-4213-82a3-1d00f6dfd04a][reflections]] on the [[id:e138b81c-6d9a-43c0-9f06-75ce69569e00][precondition]] of the [[id:dcc12dd8-1c62-4873-ac57-b4ff54a4d893][real]] as experienced by [[id:677afb73-b261-46b2-9d74-dec8fef1fd17][Thales of Miletus]] into the [[id:1552d2cd-fffe-4265-993d-07ee7c3802ea][experience of being]] of the [[id:6e32ca71-58b9-4972-a8e7-121ccd28c4df][liberal subject]].
