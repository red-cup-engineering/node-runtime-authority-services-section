The */Proto-Germanic language/* was a [[id:e8bfbd06-21f6-4e9a-a42b-27a93d5c7b21][language]] spoken between approximately [[id:9d3e5f2b-0e89-4ae8-b4e3-196fc18bdfd4][500bce]] and [[id:d20d7b3b-f4d6-487b-9f42-1df4375d84dc][200ce]] in [[id:0bfd9da0-a3d0-4920-8536-9f9fd563caf0][southern Scandinavia]] and [[id:025eb1f0-df8f-49d5-b0d6-b6e8e0e9609f][northern Germany]].

*Proto-Germanic language* developed from [[id:415d924d-3c5c-4c3d-99cf-4c3bf5c154d4][Proto-Indo-European language]], differentiated by several features, described by the following:

- [[id:2288288d-79ff-4120-b94b-acf9b7fccb8b][Grimm's Law]] indicated how consonants shifted.
- [[id:21743370-dfff-47b2-995d-8a54b41ea605][Verner's Law]] indicated exceptions to Grimm's Law.
