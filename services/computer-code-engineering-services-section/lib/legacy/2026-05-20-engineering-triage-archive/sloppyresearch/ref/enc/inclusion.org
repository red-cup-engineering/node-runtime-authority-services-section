#+title: inclusion
#+date: [2025-08-15 Fri 12:11]

*/Inclusion/* is the [[id:b4ae66ad-8700-4af3-b995-17cfd8b74eb9][condition]] [[id:c8ba8cf5-2718-43e7-9f27-126319bdfe40][induced]] by [[id:d2dd4008-f6be-48cc-a53e-cd64854f2afc][including]]. It describes whether a [[id:e5248432-6d1e-436e-9bdc-50fe0fcd3fe6][thing]] is held within what is; it is true when some thing is present within the [[id:a17b292a-0441-4c27-b672-7aa51a704f6b][domain of relating]].

** Derivation

- [[id:fc7d1ea7-e967-46b9-9fdf-1b15cbba3b0f][deriving]] [[id:bdffbe80-08e1-4e95-ad98-81cea8cf68a1][existing]] determines that a thing:
  - Be included in what it is (inducing inclusion)
  - Be [[id:f1906f33-c35e-4f1d-a02f-16d955d7e5e2][excluded]] from what it is not (inducing [[id:8d89196f-966b-4229-b99a-c77d912b0c93][exclusion]])

- The [[id:32e00173-c695-4bb6-b0d2-9dc17fec128b][act]] of including induces the condition of inclusion:
  - This condition describes the thing as a [[id:265b6725-5b20-408c-8aff-593c9d76304f][member]] of a [[id:6c2a9678-c7ae-4e69-8086-9ba85df12d75][set]].

- [[id:fc7d1ea7-e967-46b9-9fdf-1b15cbba3b0f][Deriving]] [[id:bdffbe80-08e1-4e95-ad98-81cea8cf68a1][existing]] requires that a [[id:e5248432-6d1e-436e-9bdc-50fe0fcd3fe6][thing]]:
  - Be included in what it is (inducing inclusion);
  - And be excluded from what it is not (inducing [[id:8d89196f-966b-4229-b99a-c77d912b0c93][exclusion]]).

** Derived Truths
- Every [[id:e5248432-6d1e-436e-9bdc-50fe0fcd3fe6][thing]] that [[id:bdffbe80-08e1-4e95-ad98-81cea8cf68a1][exists]] is [[id:24540b59-852d-46f7-82f2-29814d7e8f2a][conditioned]] by both [[id:efb63673-2259-401f-bf5e-906647c691f3][inclusion]] and [[id:8d89196f-966b-4229-b99a-c77d912b0c93][exclusion]].
