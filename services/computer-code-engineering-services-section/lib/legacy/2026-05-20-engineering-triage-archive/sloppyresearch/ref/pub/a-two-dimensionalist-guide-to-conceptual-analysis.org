*/A Two-Dimensionalist Guide to Conceptual Analysis/* is a [[id:caed3b82-143c-4344-a2c2-03824a83969b][book]] by [[id:547f0416-4cf7-4956-bfd6-34f573d51bfb][Jens Kipper]], [[id:88b03db0-4fed-4ace-8ef6-02ea9f2182e2][published]] as volume 25 of [[id:bc5918fa-fc43-4b89-935f-5e96ae288867][Epistemic Studies]] ([[id:98d4a424-1ec1-4b51-a9cb-4408ebb61ea6][2012]]).  It presents an [[id:56602ba2-26d1-45dd-9c93-04c098683adf][analytical framework]] for [[id:f6dbe2e2-b75e-448a-85ad-0e75c938ff53][conceptual analysis]] [[id:bc03e36a-a423-4e79-a9ec-f6909b7df428][developed]] from [[id:3e52ef07-0f81-46a6-b712-e4284bbb582e][two-dimensionalism]], a [[id:5bdf224c-3df5-4625-afe7-db8744922cba][semantic framework]] that [[id:037f0366-63ec-4c09-92fc-9c3300086c59][differentiates]] two [[id:4b914014-46a9-4e69-aed7-a3e307f25767][classes]] of [[id:a547e3dc-3cc7-4ddb-9fe4-7dfe3f96dd70][meaning]] [[id:c8ba8cf5-2718-43e7-9f27-126319bdfe40][induced]] by [[id:dd016608-457f-4c0e-a4b5-576e81322807][linguistic communication]]: [[id:2c448f19-97bd-454f-83f6-27285c8779e1][primary intension]] (descriptive reference to a priori epistemic possibilities) and [[id:43bba717-cfa8-4f77-9079-76c00fd9f586][secondary intension]] (defined reference to a posteriori metaphysical possibilities).

Kipper's central thesis is that [[id:1f9fda85-9457-42e8-8ffb-4690e21345cc][two-dimensional semantic theory]] provides a [[id:bf97ea35-5e01-4ffb-8137-fb1621c64815][methodology]] for [[id:f6dbe2e2-b75e-448a-85ad-0e75c938ff53][conceptual analysis]] across [[id:f4900586-1002-4027-b097-b6bff54312d0][epistemic]] and [[id:585e25b4-3d80-47aa-a903-0c6f219ddcf2][metaphysical modes]].

By distinguishing [[id:ac86ba89-a8ab-487b-a669-9846d8acc0b1][intension]] into two functional classes, Kipper argues that conceptual analysis can be metaphysically robust and descriptively adequate:

- primary intensions :: [[id:8b8b0529-39fa-4290-8beb-12c1567e0fbf][functions]] from [[id:34744242-074b-498c-a2e9-ef40d215d134][possible worlds]] considered as factual scenarios.
- secondary intensions :: functions from worlds considered as counterfactual scenarios.

The book applies the theory to canonical philosophical problems, including [[id:0a6f798a-4f48-4e01-a0e2-f1ae53cd80d4][necessity]], [[id:fde2d52c-548e-4520-9e82-b29206b37ade][referencing]], and [[id:4a83ac52-c6dc-4243-98a7-513b96d13b05][analyticity]]. For example, Kipper looks at [[id:a6d84255-b90d-4f57-a8af-333b4ca8a797][natural kind terms]], and how their primary intensions [[id:f117b23b-c0a2-48cc-8731-adc307ad79b0][encode]] [[id:1a4d1413-cd45-4a1c-93ab-3663d2058fbe][description]], while the secondary intensions [[id:bf3f18be-7c8a-451f-b777-29c253eca508][mapping]] [[id:2839d2a0-a607-422e-afdb-8358573bc6db][metaphysical structure]]. "Water is a clear, drinkable liquid in lakes and rivers," is a primary intension, while "water is H2O," is a secondary intension.

#+begin_aside
This is incredibly similar to the common-practice in [[id:46dab219-c7a1-4d18-8152-aeae6a92906a][LPMUDs]] of rooms having hand-written descriptions, and often only hand-written descriptions, with [[id:8cd88e03-3b82-4b23-b33f-7e25c30e1d6a][metaphysicality]] being introduced through adding optional attributes, i.e. inventories.
#+end_aside

Kipper also establishes a typology of roles in conceptual analysis:

- epistemic profile :: set of conditions under which a concept is applicable
- metaphysical anchor :: entity or property that satisfy those conditions in the actual world.

Sounds like my [[id:133d3d41-f2c6-42dc-92f8-f5d904ccf88f][relational structure]] and [[id:6f5aca7f-ffaf-4798-aa33-f7ef3ec2e43b][relational instantiation]], as much as [[id:6c38b31b-7aad-4ad7-8d7f-3597b5516978][my metaphysics]] has [[id:166ce5d3-5801-4e74-b312-6f1a902a94c1][entities]].

Kipper emphasizes the utility of two-dimensionalist conceptual analysis, especially in contexts where [[id:8578fc5b-3e79-44d4-a754-c59595b846fb][intuition]] about [[id:c41f3e80-c383-4859-915d-4268cb917bcb][possibility]] and [[id:0a6f798a-4f48-4e01-a0e2-f1ae53cd80d4][necessity]] diverge.
