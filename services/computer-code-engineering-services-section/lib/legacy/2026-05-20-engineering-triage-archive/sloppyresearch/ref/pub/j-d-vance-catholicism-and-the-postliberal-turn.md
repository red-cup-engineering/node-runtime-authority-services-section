***J.D. Vance, Catholicism, and the Postliberal Turn*** is an [essay](id:495f274d-80ec-47a2-93dc-733355ef9b9c) [authored](id:f7cc4bac-8a9d-43a2-a76f-4e014ae70405) by [Dermot Roantree](id:b6a7dc39-96af-452f-9e30-97f4ff90a7fa), published in [Studies: An Irish Quarterly Review](id:70662b3e-a2ff-46fa-9f7e-58bfbb1bedb4) volume 114, in [August, 2025](id:209258fd-3cf3-4a9f-b27e-917a56a9f328).

It seems to be a call-in from a [Catholic perspective](id:ef850d98-48cd-4466-9d3c-39b9c9ca3c2d) of [Vance](id:53093363-4792-4e93-bee4-f33c2a832486)'s misapplication of [René Girard](id:00393586-bac6-4419-9611-b78dc39e66d3)'s theory of the scapegoat to enact [Carl Schmitt](id:2f32f23e-bff5-4a84-86c7-7c6961b82b69)'s Nazism, notably a suspension of elections.
