*/Decolonization is not a Metaphor/* is an [[id:495f274d-80ec-47a2-93dc-733355ef9b9c][essay]] [[id:f7cc4bac-8a9d-43a2-a76f-4e014ae70405][authored]] by [[id:39356538-110c-450f-8174-04c3c2aaa144][Eve Tuck]] and [[id:740d65fb-e6bb-46ce-b62b-ed48e14ac014][K. Wayne Yang]] in [[id:98d4a424-1ec1-4b51-a9cb-4408ebb61ea6][2012ce]] that [[id:9ca86d85-debc-432f-b882-bbffe32a826b][analyzes]] the [[id:54b109a8-8356-4d8f-87cf-c6a7d3b54a4c][appropriation]] of "[[id:fb8b1647-1d54-49f1-adf5-396160988726][decolonization]]" in [[id:068eac91-e17d-404c-a416-7593a37f01d9][academic]] and [[id:662f9882-745d-4acf-9195-ff0b37648a1b][activist discourse]]. [cite:@TuckYang2012] They [[id:2dada8a8-7806-4ec7-8850-e1c8eb19afe6][claim]] that decolonization must refer to the [[id:c201d2c7-15fb-4f85-9f26-51cf155ee1ea][material repatriation of Indigenous being]], not a class of actions they describe as "[[id:8432ad16-0156-4fa1-9f63-870101cce3d9][settler moves to innocence]]".

#+begin_quote
Decolonization is not an "and". It is an elsewhere.
#+end_quote

- Using decolonization to describe educational reform, social justice, or shifts in consciousness dilutes its meaning and recenters settler futurity
  - settler moves to innocence
- Incommmensurable Ethic:
  - decolonization is incommensurable with civil rights or inclusivity project
    - rupture, not reform or reconciliation
- Triadic settler-colonial roles:
  - settler, native, slave
    - all can reproduce colonial logics with misapplied "decolonization"
