***A Two-Dimensionalist Guide to Conceptual Analysis*** is a [book](id:caed3b82-143c-4344-a2c2-03824a83969b) by [Jens Kipper](id:547f0416-4cf7-4956-bfd6-34f573d51bfb), [published](id:88b03db0-4fed-4ace-8ef6-02ea9f2182e2) as volume 25 of [Epistemic Studies](id:bc5918fa-fc43-4b89-935f-5e96ae288867) ([2012](id:98d4a424-1ec1-4b51-a9cb-4408ebb61ea6)). It presents an [analytical framework](id:56602ba2-26d1-45dd-9c93-04c098683adf) for [conceptual analysis](id:f6dbe2e2-b75e-448a-85ad-0e75c938ff53) [developed](id:bc03e36a-a423-4e79-a9ec-f6909b7df428) from [two-dimensionalism](id:3e52ef07-0f81-46a6-b712-e4284bbb582e), a [semantic framework](id:5bdf224c-3df5-4625-afe7-db8744922cba) that [differentiates](id:037f0366-63ec-4c09-92fc-9c3300086c59) two [classes](id:4b914014-46a9-4e69-aed7-a3e307f25767) of [meaning](id:a547e3dc-3cc7-4ddb-9fe4-7dfe3f96dd70) [induced](id:c8ba8cf5-2718-43e7-9f27-126319bdfe40) by [linguistic communication](id:dd016608-457f-4c0e-a4b5-576e81322807): [primary intension](id:2c448f19-97bd-454f-83f6-27285c8779e1) (descriptive reference to a priori epistemic possibilities) and [secondary intension](id:43bba717-cfa8-4f77-9079-76c00fd9f586) (defined reference to a posteriori metaphysical possibilities).

Kipper's central thesis is that [two-dimensional semantic theory](id:1f9fda85-9457-42e8-8ffb-4690e21345cc) provides a [methodology](id:bf97ea35-5e01-4ffb-8137-fb1621c64815) for [conceptual analysis](id:f6dbe2e2-b75e-448a-85ad-0e75c938ff53) across [epistemic](id:f4900586-1002-4027-b097-b6bff54312d0) and [metaphysical modes](id:585e25b4-3d80-47aa-a903-0c6f219ddcf2).

By distinguishing [intension](id:ac86ba89-a8ab-487b-a669-9846d8acc0b1) into two functional classes, Kipper argues that conceptual analysis can be metaphysically robust and descriptively adequate:

primary intensions  
[functions](id:8b8b0529-39fa-4290-8beb-12c1567e0fbf) from [possible worlds](id:34744242-074b-498c-a2e9-ef40d215d134) considered as factual scenarios.

secondary intensions  
functions from worlds considered as counterfactual scenarios.

The book applies the theory to canonical philosophical problems, including [necessity](id:0a6f798a-4f48-4e01-a0e2-f1ae53cd80d4), [referencing](id:fde2d52c-548e-4520-9e82-b29206b37ade), and [analyticity](id:4a83ac52-c6dc-4243-98a7-513b96d13b05). For example, Kipper looks at [natural kind terms](id:a6d84255-b90d-4f57-a8af-333b4ca8a797), and how their primary intensions [encode](id:f117b23b-c0a2-48cc-8731-adc307ad79b0) [description](id:1a4d1413-cd45-4a1c-93ab-3663d2058fbe), while the secondary intensions [mapping](id:bf3f18be-7c8a-451f-b777-29c253eca508) [metaphysical structure](id:2839d2a0-a607-422e-afdb-8358573bc6db). "Water is a clear, drinkable liquid in lakes and rivers," is a primary intension, while "water is H2O," is a secondary intension.

Kipper also establishes a typology of roles in conceptual analysis:

epistemic profile  
set of conditions under which a concept is applicable

metaphysical anchor  
entity or property that satisfy those conditions in the actual world.

Sounds like my [relational structure](id:133d3d41-f2c6-42dc-92f8-f5d904ccf88f) and [relational instantiation](id:6f5aca7f-ffaf-4798-aa33-f7ef3ec2e43b), as much as [my metaphysics](id:6c38b31b-7aad-4ad7-8d7f-3597b5516978) has [entities](id:166ce5d3-5801-4e74-b312-6f1a902a94c1).

Kipper emphasizes the utility of two-dimensionalist conceptual analysis, especially in contexts where [intuition](id:8578fc5b-3e79-44d4-a754-c59595b846fb) about [possibility](id:c41f3e80-c383-4859-915d-4268cb917bcb) and [necessity](id:0a6f798a-4f48-4e01-a0e2-f1ae53cd80d4) diverge.
