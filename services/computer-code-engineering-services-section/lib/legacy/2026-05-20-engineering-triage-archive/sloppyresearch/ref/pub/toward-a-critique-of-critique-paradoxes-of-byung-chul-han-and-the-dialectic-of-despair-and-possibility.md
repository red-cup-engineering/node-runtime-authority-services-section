***Toward a Critique of Critique: Paradoxes of Byung-Chul Han and the Dialectic of Despair and Possibility*** is an [text article](id:c56f3e98-22de-4bdd-9136-15a6f5889e1a) by [İbrahim Berkan Karataş](id:a6886f15-1a59-417d-98e1-8d10d0e70ed2) that looks at the work of [Byung-Chul Han](id:9bbd19ff-9564-48be-aeb5-ace10cfcad7f).

Notable because Han and I think about a lot of similar topics, but with different approaches and different ideological backings.

# Readings

## Reading \#1

Excited to read through this piece, since there's so much overlap between what [Byung-Chul Han](id:9bbd19ff-9564-48be-aeb5-ace10cfcad7f) and I write about, but we're coming at it from very different positions and orientations. Practically, it's going to help me catch up on some of the latest theories that are most relevant to [my intellectual practices](id:d2ca89aa-c9dc-4213-be93-525ed5954185), especially [my files](id:3ffa600c-f831-428f-bd11-a4e6583ff5ad), [my website](id:cc253409-9a82-4c5d-a4cd-8013f4e350c6), and this form of [digital gardening](id:04bb8f02-8fed-4180-9e80-5a745a4e465d) that I'm doing; what I've called before [vernacular autoethnosis](id:98f833f3-82d9-4e83-86be-9fd6ed124027), but I suspect I will [coin a new term](id:6f2fa75a-9118-470d-8d4c-fc6bcef30c55) sometime, [influenced](id:a9f58f35-38d6-4309-8958-c5c5f209738f) by readings like this.
