* Section 1 — Posetal Prototype

** 1.1 Complete Heyting algebra and nuclei
Data: a complete Heyting algebra \(H=(H,\le,\wedge,\vee,\Rightarrow,\bot,\top)\).  
Define negation \(\neg x := (x \Rightarrow \bot)\).

*** Definition 1.1 (nucleus / closure operator)
A map \(c:H\to H\) is a nucleus if it is monotone, inflationary, idempotent, and meet-preserving:
\[
x\le y\Rightarrow c(x)\le c(y),\quad x\le c(x),\quad c(c(x))=c(x),\quad c(x\wedge y)=c(x)\wedge c(y).
\]

*** Definition 1.2 (interior)
\(i(x):=\neg c(\neg x)\).  
Then \(i\) is monotone, deflationary, idempotent, and join-preserving.

*** Lemma 1.3 (fixpoints form a Heyting subalgebra)
\(\mathrm{Fix}(c)=\{x\in H\mid c(x)=x\}\) is a complete Heyting subalgebra of \(H\).

*Proof sketch.*  
Meet-preservation and idempotence give closure under arbitrary meets.  
Use \(i\) for joins via De Morgan duality; implication restricts by adjunction.

*** Lemma 1.4 (stabilization inequality)
For all \(x\in H\), \(i(x)\le x\le c(x)\).  
Periodic points of \(c\) are exactly \(\mathrm{Fix}(c)\).

---

** 1.2 Relations internal to \(H\)
Let \(H_c:=\mathrm{Fix}(c)\).

*** Definition 1.5 (Relational category \(\mathbf{Rel}(H_c)\))
- Objects: elements \(a\in H_c\).
- Morphisms \(a\to b\): elements \(r\in H\) with \(r\le (a\Rightarrow b)\).
- Identity: \(1_a:= (a\Rightarrow a)\).
- Composition: for \(r:a\to b,\, s:b\to z\),
  \[
  s\circ r := r\wedge (a\Rightarrow s) \le (a\Rightarrow z).
  \]

*Associativity proof sketch.*  
Residuation in a Heyting algebra ensures  
\((t\circ s)\circ r = t\wedge (a\Rightarrow (s\wedge (a\Rightarrow r))) = t\circ (s\circ r)\).  
Unitality follows from \(a\Rightarrow( a\Rightarrow b)=a\Rightarrow b\).

If closure-stability is desired, postcompose with \(c\):  
\(s\circ r:=c(r\wedge (a\Rightarrow s))\).

---

** 1.3 Dynamics via a KZ-comonad

*** Definition 1.6 (composite modality)
\(G:=i\circ c:H\to H\).

*** Lemma 1.7 (KZ-comonad structure)
There exist \(\varepsilon_x:Gx\le x\) and \(\delta_x:Gx\le GGx\) natural in \(x\)
making \((G,\varepsilon,\delta)\) a comonad on the poset \(H\).

*Proof sketch.*  
Posetal comonads reduce to inequalities.  
Use \(i\le \mathrm{id}\le c\) and the idempotence of both operators.

*** Definition 1.8 (coalgebra)
A coalgebra for \(G\) is \(x\in H\) with \(\gamma:x\le Gx\) satisfying the usual
Kleisli coherence inequalities.

*** Proposition 1.9 (self-coherence criterion)
\(x\) is a \(G\)-coalgebra iff \(x\le i(c(x))\).

*Proof sketch.*  
In posets, a structure map exists iff the inequality holds;  
the comonad laws reduce to idempotence of \(i,c\).

* Section 2 — Two Commuting Nuclei and Factorization

** 2.1 Data and composites
Data: two nuclei \(c_f,c_b:H\to H\) with \(c_fc_b=c_bc_f\).  
Let \(i_f,i_b\) be interiors. Define:
\[
C:=i_f\circ c_b,\qquad M:=i_b\circ c_f.
\]

*** Lemma 2.1
\(C\) and \(M\) are nuclei; \(CM=MC\).

*Proof sketch.*  
In a Heyting algebra, interiors preserve joins, closures preserve meets;  
commutation of \(c_f,c_b\) and De Morgan duality give the claim.

---

** 2.2 Orthogonal factorization
*** Definition 2.2
A map \(x\le y\) is \(C\)-stable if \(x\le Cy\); \(M\)-stable if \(Mx\le y\).

*** Proposition 2.3 (factorization)
Every \(x\le y\) factors as \(x \le C(y) \le y\),
with the first map \(C\)-stable and the second \(M\)-stable.

*Proof sketch.*  
Use \(x\le y\le c_by\Rightarrow i_f(c_by)=Cy\).  
For the right leg, \(M(Cy)\le y\) because \(M\le \mathrm{id}\) on right argument; check by adjunction.

* Section 3 — Transfinite Closure Tower

** 3.1 Construction
Define recursively:
\[
H_0:=H,\quad H_{\alpha+1}:=\mathrm{Fix}(c_\alpha),\quad
H_\lambda:=\bigvee_{\beta<\lambda} H_\beta\ (\text{for limit } \lambda).
\]

*** Lemma 3.1 (monotonicity)
\(H_{\alpha+1}\subseteq H_\alpha\).

*** Proposition 3.2 (finite bound)
If \(H\) is finite, the chain stabilizes in \(\le|H|\) steps.

*** Theorem 3.3 (accessibility-stabilization)
If each \(c_\alpha\) is accessible, there exists a regular \(\lambda\) with \(H_\lambda=H_{\lambda+1}\).

*Proof sketch.*  
Standard Adámek–Rosický argument on accessible reflective subcategories
implies stabilization by cardinal bounds.

---

** 3.2 Toy model
Let \(E=\{0<1<2\}\), \(H=\mathcal P(E)\).  
Define \(c_f(U)=\uparrow U,\ c_b(U)=\downarrow U\).  
Compute \(C=i_f\circ c_b,\ M=i_b\circ c_f\), frontier \(\partial(U)=c_f(U)\cap\neg i_f(U)\).  
Iterate tower: stabilizes in \(\le 8\) steps.

* Section 4 — The Presheaf Cell \( \mathcal E=\mathrm{PSh}(\{0\to1\}) \)

** 4.1 Components
An object \(X=(X_0,X_1,u^\ast:X_1\to X_0)\).  
Products, limits, and subobjects computed componentwise.

---

** 4.2 Two nuclei
*** Forward topology \(j_f\)
Sieves \(S_f(0)=\{\mathrm{id}_0,u\},\ S_f(1)=\{\mathrm{id}_1\}\).  
Sheaves: objects with \(u^\ast\) an effective epi.

*** Backward topology \(j_b\)
Sieves \(S_b(1)=\{\mathrm{id}_1,u^{op}\},\ S_b(0)=\{\mathrm{id}_0\}\).  
Sheaves: objects with \(u^\ast\) a mono.

*** Lemma 4.1 (local operator axioms)
\(j_f,j_b\) are inflationary, idempotent, stable under pullback, and have local character.

*Proof sketch.*  
Finite site: verify directly on \(\Omega(0),\Omega(1)\), and pullback along \(u\).

*** Lemma 4.2 (commutation)
\(j_f j_b=j_b j_f\).

*Proof sketch.*  
Evaluate on \(\Omega(0),\Omega(1)\);  
only two components and one restriction map, explicit case check yields equality.

---

** 4.3 Reflectors and inclusions
For \(X=(X_0,X_1,u^\ast)\):
\[
(a_f X)_1 = X_1,\ (a_f X)_0=\mathrm{coeq}(X_1\rightrightarrows X_0),\quad
(a_b X)_0 = X_0,\ (a_b X)_1=\mathrm{eq}(X_1\rightrightarrows X_0).
\]

*** Proposition 4.4 (essentiality)
The inclusions \(i_f,i_b\) are essential: each has both left and right adjoints.

*Proof sketch.*  
Right adjoints exist since subtopoi are defined by pointwise exactness constraints
preserved by limits; construct them as right Kan extensions.

*** Corollary 4.5 (adjoint chain)
\[
\Pi^{\to}=a_f,\quad \Box=i_f,\quad \Diamond=i_b,\quad \nabla^{\leftarrow}=\Pi_b
\]
forming \(\Pi^{\to}\dashv\Box\dashv\Diamond\dashv\nabla^{\leftarrow}\).

---

* Section 5 — Modal (Co)monads and Endofunctor Factorization

** 5.1 Modal composites
\(C:=\Box\Diamond\), \(M:=\Diamond\Box\).

*** Lemma 5.2 (exactness)
In \(\mathrm{PSh}(\{0\to1\})\), \(C\) preserves finite limits, \(M\) finite colimits.

*Proof sketch.*  
\(i_f,i_b\) left exact; \(a_f,a_b\) preserve (co)limits as (co)reflectors.

---

** 5.2 Factorization on endofunctors
\[
\mathrm{End}_\Box(\mathcal E)=\{G\mid G i_f\simeq i_f G\},\quad
\mathrm{End}_\Diamond(\mathcal E)=\{G\mid G i_b\simeq i_b G\}.
\]

*** Proposition 5.4 (reflective/coreflective)
Precomposition with \(i_f\) has left adjoint \(a_f\circ-\);  
precomposition with \(i_b\) has right adjoint \(-\circ\Pi_b\).

*** Corollary 5.5 (comparison)
Each \(G\) has comparison maps:
\[
\eta^f_G: G \Rightarrow i_f a_f G,\quad \epsilon^b_G: a_b i_b G \Rightarrow G.
\]

* Appendix — Componentwise Calculations

** A.1 Sheafification and inclusion
- \(a_f\): \((a_f X)_1 = X_1\); \((a_f X)_0 = \mathrm{coeq}(X_1\rightrightarrows X_0)\).
- \(i_f\): inclusion of objects with \(u^\ast\) epi.
- \(\Pi_f\): right adjoint forcing \(u^\ast\) epi internally.
- \(a_b\): \((a_b X)_0 = X_0\); \((a_b X)_1=\mathrm{eq}(X_1\rightrightarrows X_0)\).
- \(i_b\): inclusion of objects with \(u^\ast\) mono.
- \(\Pi_b\): right adjoint making \(u^\ast\) mono (kernel-pair quotient).

---

** A.2 Commutation check
On \(\Omega(0),\Omega(1)\): explicit case analysis yields \(j_fj_b=j_bj_f\).

---

** A.3 Adjunction verification
For \(X\in\mathcal E, Y\in \mathrm{Sh}_f\),
\[
\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Sh}_f}(a_f X, Y)\cong \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal E}(X, i_f Y).
\]

*Proof sketch.*  
A map \(a_f X\to Y\) is a pair commuting with \(u^\ast\).  
By the coequalizer property, this corresponds to a natural map \(X\to i_fY\).

---

** A.4 Modal composites
\[
(\Box\Diamond X)_1 \simeq X_1,\quad
(\Box\Diamond X)_0 \simeq \mathrm{coeq}(\mathrm{eq}(X_1\rightrightarrows X_0)\rightrightarrows X_0).
\]
Applying twice yields an isomorphic result → idempotence.

---

** A.5 Orthogonal factorization
For \(f:X\to Y\):
\[
X \xrightarrow{\eta^f_X} \Box\Diamond X \xrightarrow{\Box\Diamond f} \Box\Diamond Y \xrightarrow{\epsilon^b_Y} Y.
\]
Left leg \(\Box\)-stable, right leg \(\Diamond\)-stable.

---

** Example
Let \(X_0=\{a,b\}, X_1=\{*\}, u^\ast(*)=a\).  
Compute \(a_fX, a_bX\); verify \(\Box\Diamond X\) idempotence componentwise.
