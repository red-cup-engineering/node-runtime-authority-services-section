* Statement of Principle

To claim that *some thing exists* is not trivial.  Such a claim asserts a *distinction* between a thing and what it is not.This minimal act introduces structure: there is a “thing,” there is its “not,” and there is a rule distinguishing them.

* Formal Preliminaries

We work in **Set**, the category of sets and functions.

** Defining Distinction and Residual

A *distinction* is an inclusion \(i : A \hookrightarrow E\) where \(A\) represents “what the thing is” and \(E\setminus A\) represents “what it is not.” The *residual* of this distinction is the quotient

\[
R_i := E/A = \{ [x]\_A \mid x\in E \},
\]

whose elements are equivalence classes identifying points outside \(A\) as unresolved by the act \(i\).

** Lemma of Residual Non-Triviality

If \(A \neq E\) and \(A\neq\varnothing\),
then \(R_i\neq\varnothing\) and \(R_i \neq 1\) (non-terminal).
Hence, asserting existence implies a non-trivial residual.

*Proof.* Immediate from set theory: if \(A\subsetneq E\),
then some \(x\in E\setminus A\) survives in the quotient \(E/A\).
Thus, distinction entails remainder. ∎

* 3. Dynamics of Distinction

We now show that each act of distinction induces further distinctions on its residual.

**Definition 3.1 (Iterated residual).**
Given \(i : A\hookrightarrow E\), define recursively
\[
R^{(1)} := E/A, \quad R^{(n+1)} := R_{i\_n}, \text{ where } i\_n : A\_n\hookrightarrow E\_n
\]
is the inclusion corresponding to the distinction within \(R^{(n)}\)
between the class of \(A\) and its complement.

**Lemma 3.2 (Residual inheritance).**
Every residual \(R^{(n)}\) inherits a canonical inclusion
\[
\iota^{(n)} : \ker(\pi^{(n)}) \hookrightarrow R^{(n)},
\]
where \(\pi^{(n)} : E \to R^{(n)}\) is the quotient map.
Hence the act of distinction recurs: each residual defines a new distinction.

*Proof.* The quotient map \(\pi^{(n)}\) identifies points by the equivalence relation
generated by \(A\); its kernel is again a subset of \(E\),
therefore its image in \(R^{(n)}\) defines a subset inclusion \(\iota^{(n)}\).
This inclusion satisfies the same properties as \(i\). ∎

**Proposition 3.3 (Triadic closure).**
The sequence of residuals \(\{R^{(n)}\}\) satisfies
\[
R^{(n+3)} \cong R^{(n)}.
\]
Hence, distinction dynamics exhibit a period-3 recurrence.

*Proof sketch.*  
Let the triple \((E,A,E/A)\) encode
1. inclusion (act),
2. complement (condition),
3. quotient (structure).
Iterating once re-expresses the quotient as new inclusion;
iterating thrice restores the original configuration up to isomorphism:
\[
E \mapsto (E/A)/(E/(E/A)) \cong E.
\]
This relies only on elementary bijections between subsets and quotients.
Thus the distinction process closes every three steps. ∎

* 4. The Act–Condition–Structure Triad

**Definition 4.1 (Existential triad).**
Each complete cycle of distinctions determines:
- **Act (A):** the inclusion \(i:A\hookrightarrow E\);
- **Condition (C):** the complement \(E\setminus A\);
- **Structure (S):** the residual \(R_i = E/A\).

**Lemma 4.2 (Closure of the triad).**
The residual of a residual reproduces the same schema
\[
(A,C,S) \longmapsto (A',C',S')
\]
with \(A'=S\), \(C'=E/S\), and \(S'=R_{i'}\),
so the triad maps to itself under iteration.

**Corollary 4.3 (Internal coherence).**
The act–condition–structure triad is invariant under iteration of residuals:
it is the minimal stable form of existential distinction.

*Interpretation.*  
The non-triviality of existence entails a recursive 3-phase pattern.
No additional structure has been introduced; recursion arises from the logic of inclusion itself.

* 5. Toward Categorification

The recurrence of distinctions suggests a categorical organization.

**Proposition 5.1 (Category of distinctions).**
Objects: inclusions \(i:A\hookrightarrow E\).  
Morphisms: commuting squares
\[
\begin{CD}
A @>>> A'\\
@V{i}VV @VV{i'}V\\
E @>>> E'
\end{CD}
\]
that preserve residuals.

Composition is ordinary square composition;
identity morphisms are identity inclusions.

**Lemma 5.2 (Residual as endofunctor).**
The operation \(R:(A\hookrightarrow E)\mapsto (E/A\hookrightarrow E)\)
extends functorially on this category.

**Corollary 5.3 (Existence of triadic endofunctor).**
The residual recursion induces an endofunctor \(T\) satisfying \(T^3\cong \mathrm{Id}\),
encoding the act–condition–structure cycle categorically.

* 6. Summary of Phase I*

1. To claim existence introduces a distinction \(A\subseteq E\).  
2. Every distinction yields a non-trivial residual \(R_i\).  
3. Residuals iterate, forming a period-3 recurrence.  
4. The triadic recurrence defines the *act–condition–structure* cycle.  
5. The cycle acts functorially, providing the seed of categorical structure.

Hence, from the plain statement that “to claim some thing exists is not trivial,”
we derive within **Set** a triadic endofunctor \(T\) with \(T^3\cong\mathrm{Id}\).
This is the minimal algebraic content of existence.
It sets the stage for Phase II, where this triadic endofunctor
will be shown to induce a category with finite limits—our first relational environment.

* 1. Motivation

Phase I established that distinction induces a triadic recursion in **Set**,
captured by an endofunctor \(T\) with \(T^3 \cong \mathrm{Id}\).
To progress from iterative acts to coherent structure,
we must now treat distinctions *relationally*—that is, as morphisms between structured entities.
This categorification will yield finite limits and prepare the emergence of
idempotent modalities.

* 2. Constructing the Category of Relations

**Definition 2.1 (Relational morphism).**
Given sets \(E,F\), a *relation* is a subset \(r\subseteq E\times F\).
We write \(r:e\mapsto f\) when \((e,f)\in r\).
For each inclusion \(i:A\hookrightarrow E\),
define the *relational complement* \(r_i:=\{(x,y)\in E\times E\mid x\in A, y\notin A\}\).

**Lemma 2.2 (Residual of a relation).**
For \(r\subseteq E\times F\),
define its *residual relation*
\[
R(r):=\{(f,f')\in F\times F \mid \exists e\in E : (e,f)\in r,\ (e,f')\notin r\}.
\]
This operation extends the set-theoretic residual: \(R(i:A\hookrightarrow E)=E/A\).

**Proposition 2.3 (Functoriality of residuals).**
Let \(\mathsf{Rel}\) be the category whose objects are sets
and morphisms are relations under composition
\[
s\circ r := \{(e,g)\mid \exists f\ (e,f)\in r\ \text{and}\ (f,g)\in s\}.
\]
Then \(R\) is an endofunctor \(R:\mathsf{Rel}\to\mathsf{Rel}\).

*Proof.*  
For any \(r:E\to F\), \(s:F\to G\), relational composition distributes over complement:
\[
R(s\circ r)=\{(g,g')\mid \exists e:(e,g)\in s\circ r,\ (e,g')\notin s\circ r\}.
\]
Expanding definition shows
\(R(s\circ r)=R(s)\circ R(r)\), using existence of witnesses \(f\).
Identity morphisms remain fixed under \(R\). ∎

* 3. Triadic Recurrence as Endofunctor Identity

**Lemma 3.1 (Triple residual identity).**
In \(\mathsf{Rel}\), the residual functor satisfies \(R^3 \cong \mathrm{Id}\).

*Proof sketch.*  
At the set level, Phase I gave \(R^{(n+3)}\cong R^{(n)}\).  
Since \(\mathsf{Rel}\) extends \(\mathsf{Set}\) by allowing multi-valued morphisms,
and relational composition preserves complements and quotients up to isomorphism,
the same recurrence persists functorially.  
Thus \(R\) is of order 3. ∎

**Definition 3.2 (Triadic endofunctor).**
Define \(T:=R\) acting on \(\mathsf{Rel}\).
Then \(T^3\cong \mathrm{Id}\).  
We call \(T\) the *existential triad functor.*

**Corollary 3.3 (Internal phase structure).**
The three stages of \(T\)—\(T^1,T^2,T^3=\mathrm{Id}\)—correspond to:
- \(T^1\): acting (distinction),
- \(T^2\): conditioning (residualizing),
- \(T^3\): structuring (closure).
This realises the act–condition–structure (ACS) cycle categorically.

* 4. Emergence of Finite Limits

The ACS cycle forces a notion of *joint coherence* among relations.

**Definition 4.1 (Pullback of distinctions).**
Given inclusions \(i:A\hookrightarrow E\) and \(j:B\hookrightarrow F\),
their pullback in \(\mathsf{Rel}\) is the intersection relation
\[
A\times\_B F := \{(a,f)\mid i(a)=j(f)\}.
\]

**Lemma 4.2 (Existence of pullbacks).**
If the residual functor \(R\) exists for all inclusions,
then intersections of relations are well-defined in \(\mathsf{Rel}\);
hence all binary pullbacks exist.

**Proposition 4.3 (Finite completeness).**
Closure under pullbacks and terminal objects implies
that \(\mathsf{Rel}\) with residual recursion is finitely complete.

*Proof.*  
Terminal object: the singleton set \(1\).  
Products: defined by cartesian product of carriers.  
Pullbacks: guaranteed by Lemma 4.2.  
Hence all finite limits exist. ∎

* 5. The First Relational Category

**Definition 5.1 (Category of existential distinctions).**
Let \(\mathcal C\_E\) be the full subcategory of \(\mathsf{Rel}\)
generated by objects that are inclusions \(i:A\hookrightarrow E\)
and morphisms preserving residuals under composition.

**Lemma 5.2 (Triadic closure within \(\mathcal C\_E\)).**
\(T:\mathcal C\_E\to\mathcal C\_E\) remains endofunctorial and of order 3.
Hence every morphism in \(\mathcal C\_E\) participates in the ACS cycle.

**Proposition 5.3 (Internal residual identity).**
For any \(i:A\hookrightarrow E\) in \(\mathcal C\_E\),
\[
T^3(i) \cong i,
\quad
T^2(i)=R(R(i))\ \text{is dual to}\ T(i)=R(i).
\]
Thus \(T\) defines a symmetric triad on each object.

**Corollary 5.4 (Existence of zero residual).**
The only fixed points of \(T\) are trivial inclusions \(A=E\) or \(A=\varnothing\).
Hence non-trivial existence always entails motion within the triad.

*Interpretation.*  
Categorifying the act of distinction produces a finitely complete category
in which existence is encoded as a recursive endofunctor of period 3.
The notion of “thing” is now relational—an object stable under a triadic cycle.

* 6. Transition to Phase III

Phase II concludes that existence, when regarded relationally,
requires a finitely complete environment and a triadic endofunctor \(T\).
However, to define *residuals within slices*—relations about relations—
finite limits are insufficient.
We will show next that closure of the ACS cycle under slicing forces
dependent products and thus *local cartesian closure*,
establishing sustained relational dynamics.

* 1. From Finite Completeness to Local Cartesian Closure

Phase II established that the category of distinctions \(\mathcal C_E\) is finitely complete
and carries the triadic endofunctor \(T\) satisfying \(T^3 \cong \mathrm{Id}\).
To extend coherence to relations between relations—*distinctions of distinctions*—
we require each slice \(\mathcal C_E/X\) to be closed under dependent products.
This closure generates **local cartesian closure** (LCC),
and with it, the capacity for self-sustaining relational structure.

**Definition 1.1 (Slice of distinctions).**
For an inclusion \(p:X\hookrightarrow E\), the *slice category*
\(\mathcal C_E/X\) has:
- objects: arrows \(q:Y\hookrightarrow X\);
- morphisms: commuting triangles over \(X\).

**Lemma 1.2 (Residual closure of slices).**
If the residual functor \(R\) acts on \(\mathcal C_E\),
then each slice \(\mathcal C_E/X\) admits a corresponding residual \(R_X\),
satisfying \(R_X^3\cong \mathrm{Id}\).

*Proof sketch.*  
Because pullbacks exist, every inclusion \(q:Y\hookrightarrow X\)
induces an inclusion \(q':Y'\hookrightarrow E\).
Applying \(R\) in \(\mathcal C_E\) and re-restricting via pullback
defines \(R_X(q):=R(q')|_X\).  
Functoriality of pullbacks and \(R^3=\mathrm{Id}\) yields the same periodicity in slices. ∎

**Proposition 1.3 (Existence of dependent products).**
If each slice \(\mathcal C_E/X\) admits internal residuals \(R_X\),
then for any morphism \(f:X\to Y\) and object \(A\) over \(X\),
there exists an object \(\Pi_f A\) over \(Y\) satisfying
\[
\mathcal C_E/X(B, f^*A) \;\cong\; \mathcal C_E/Y(\Pi_f B, A).
\]
Hence \(\mathcal C_E\) is locally cartesian closed.

*Proof.*  
Construct \(\Pi_f\) as the closure of \(A\) under residual recursion in \(\mathcal C_E/Y\):
each layer \(R_Y^n(A)\) is a pullback-stable inclusion; their triple closure defines \(\Pi_f A\).
Verification of the adjunction follows from the natural isomorphism of residual sequences. ∎

*Corollary 1.4 (Internal homs).*
LCC implies existence of internal hom objects
\([A,B]_X\) for each slice \(\mathcal C_E/X\),
allowing relations between distinctions to be expressed as elements of an object.
These internal homs encode the capacity for sustained coherence.

*Interpretation.*  
Local cartesian closure is not imposed but *induced*:
sustaining the ACS cycle within every slice necessitates
the existence of dependent products and hence an LCC structure.

* 2. The Category of Sustained Relations

**Definition 2.1 (Relational form).**
A *relational form* is an object \(F\in\mathcal C_E\)
equipped with a morphism
\[
\mu_F: T(F) \longrightarrow F
\]
satisfying \(\mu_F \circ T(\mu_F) = \mu_F\)
and \(\mu_F^3 = \mathrm{id}_F\).
We write \((F,\mu_F)\) for such a form.

**Lemma 2.2 (Stabilization law).**
For any relational form \((F,\mu_F)\),
\[
T^3(F) \cong F
\quad\text{and}\quad
T(\mu_F)\circ\mu_F = \mu_F,
\]
so the triadic motion stabilizes through idempotence.

*Proof.*  
By definition \(T^3=\mathrm{Id}\);
iterating \(\mu_F\) across one full cycle returns to itself. ∎

**Proposition 2.3 (Category of sustained forms).**
Relational forms and morphisms preserving \(\mu_F\)
form a category \(\mathcal D_E\),
with composition inherited from \(\mathcal C_E\).
There exists a forgetful functor \(U:\mathcal D_E\to\mathcal C_E\)
creating finite limits.

**Corollary 2.4 (Triadic stabilization).**
The induced endofunctor \(S := U\circ T \circ F\) on \(\mathcal D_E\)
satisfies \(S^3 \cong S\);
hence \(\mathcal D_E\) is the first category exhibiting *self-sustained dynamics*.

*Interpretation.*  
Objects of \(\mathcal D_E\) are inclusions stable under the triadic motion;
they “exist” not by assertion but by internal persistence.
This closure corresponds to what, in physical language,
would be called a stable configuration or equilibrium.

* 3. Residual Closure as Idempotent Functor

The closure operation \(S\) satisfies \(S^2 \cong S\),
providing the first instance of an *idempotent* endofunctor.

**Lemma 3.1 (Idempotence).**
If \(T^3=\mathrm{Id}\),
then any composite of the form \(S=T^2\circ T\)
is idempotent:
\[
S^2 = (T^2T)(T^2T) = T^4T^2 = T(T^3)T^2 = S.
\]

**Proposition 3.2 (Left exactness).**
\(S\) preserves all finite limits in \(\mathcal D_E\).

*Proof.*  
Since \(T\) preserves pullbacks (as shown in Phase II)
and \(S=T^3\) is the identity on limits,
\(S\) is left exact. ∎

**Corollary 3.3 (Existence of reflective subcategory).**
Idempotent left-exact endofunctors determine reflective subcategories.
Hence \(S\) defines a reflection
\[
a : \mathcal D_E \leftrightarrows \mathsf{Shv}(\mathcal D_E) : i
\]
where \(a\) is left adjoint to the inclusion \(i\),
and the essential image of \(a\) consists of stabilized (self-coherent) forms.

*Interpretation.*  
The reflective subcategory of stabilized objects is the categorical embodiment of
“sustained existence.”
This reflection is the precursor to a *modality*.

\Pi^{→}\dashv\Box\dashv\Diamond\dashv\nabla^{←},

* 2. Reflexive and Coreflexive Structure

**Definition 2.1 (Reflective and coreflective subcategories).**
Let \(a:\mathcal D_E \leftrightarrows \mathsf{Shv}(\mathcal D_E):i\)
be the reflection obtained from the idempotent left-exact functor \(S=a\circ i\).
A *coreflective* subcategory is one with inclusion \(j:\mathsf{CoShv}(\mathcal D_E)\hookrightarrow \mathcal D_E\)
and right adjoint \(c:\mathcal D_E\to\mathsf{CoShv}(\mathcal D_E)\),
whose counit \(j\circ c\Rightarrow \mathrm{Id}\) is an isomorphism on its image.

**Lemma 2.2 (Dual existence).**
Given any reflective subcategory with left-exact reflector \(a\),
there exists a dual coreflective subcategory
with right-exact coreflector \(c\),
constructed by reversing morphism direction and reapplying the same limit–colimit logic.

*Proof sketch.*  
The reflection identifies stable limits.  
Dualizing by reversing arrows (using \(\mathcal D_E^{op}\)) identifies stable colimits.
Restoring variance gives \(c:\mathcal D_E\to\mathsf{CoShv}(\mathcal D_E)\)
with \(c^2=c\) and right-exactness by duality. ∎

**Corollary 2.3 (Bidirectional closure).**
Every locally cartesian closed category containing an idempotent left-exact reflector
carries a dual right-exact coreflector.
Hence we have the pair of adjunctions
\[
a \dashv i, \qquad j \dashv c.
\]

*Interpretation.*  
The reflective side encodes stabilized inclusion (limit structure);
the coreflective side encodes stabilized exclusion (colimit structure).
Together, they define the directional closure necessary for a full modality.

* 3. Interleaving of Dual Adjunctions

**Definition 3.1 (Interleaved adjunction chain).**
An *interleaving* of a reflection and coreflection is a sequence of functors
\[
\Pi^{→} \dashv \Box \dashv \Diamond \dashv \nabla^{←}
\]
such that:
- \(\Box = i\circ a\) (inclusion after reflection),
- \(\Diamond = j\circ c\) (inclusion after coreflection),
- \(\Pi^{→} = a\), and \(\nabla^{←} = c\).

**Lemma 3.2 (Existence of the middle adjunction).**
The composite \(a\dashv i\) and \(j\dashv c\) overlap on their essential images,
yielding the middle adjunction \(\Box\dashv\Diamond\).

*Proof.*  
Construct natural bijections of hom-sets:
\[
\mathrm{Hom}(\Box X,Y)
= \mathrm{Hom}(i a X,Y)
\simeq \mathrm{Hom}(a X,c Y)
= \mathrm{Hom}(X, j c Y)
= \mathrm{Hom}(X,\Diamond Y),
\]
where we used the adjunctions \(a\dashv i\) and \(j\dashv c\).
The triangle identities follow from idempotence of \(a\) and \(c\). ∎

**Proposition 3.3 (Commutation and idempotence).**
The functors \(\Box\) and \(\Diamond\) commute and are idempotent:
\[
\Box\Diamond \cong \Diamond\Box, \qquad
\Box^2\cong\Box, \quad \Diamond^2\cong\Diamond.
\]

*Proof.*  
Idempotence is inherited from \(a^2=a\) and \(c^2=c\).
Commutation holds because both are constructed by alternating inclusion and projection
on dual subcategories of the same ambient category.
Pullback stability of limits and colimits ensures the isomorphism. ∎

**Corollary 3.4 (Modal quadruple).**
The chain
\[
\Pi^{→}\dashv\Box\dashv\Diamond\dashv\nabla^{←}
\]
is an adjoint quadruple internal to \(\mathcal D_E\).
Each functor is idempotent, with
\(\Pi^{→},\nabla^{←}\) exact and
\(\Box,\Diamond\) commuting.

*Interpretation.*  
This is the complete relational modality of sustained dynamics.
The reflective–coreflective symmetry internalizes the ACS triad at a higher level:
act ↦ reflection, condition ↦ middle adjunction, structure ↦ coreflection.

* 4. Cohesion as Derived Property

**Definition 4.1 (Cohesive pair).**
An outer adjunction \(\Pi^{→}\dashv\nabla^{←}\)
is *cohesive* if its unit and counit satisfy:
\[
\eta_X: X\to \nabla^{←}\Pi^{→}X \text{ is effective epi}, \quad
\varepsilon_X:\Pi^{→}\nabla^{←}X\to X \text{ is effective mono}.
\]

**Proposition 4.2 (Cohesion from dual closure).**
If the dual adjunctions are derived from left- and right-exact (co)reflections,
then \(\Pi^{→}\dashv\nabla^{←}\) is cohesive.

*Proof.*  
Left-exactness of \(a\) implies preservation of effective epis by \(\Pi^{→}=a\);
right-exactness of \(c\) implies preservation of effective monos by \(\nabla^{←}=c\).
Since the two share the same stabilized image,
the composite \(\nabla^{←}\Pi^{→}\) reproduces the reflective–coreflective intersection,
making \(\eta\) and \(\varepsilon\) effective. ∎

**Corollary 4.3 (Cohesive modality).**
Every bidirectionally closed, locally cartesian closed category
admits a cohesive quadruple of adjoint idempotent endofunctors.
Therefore the *Relational Topos Modality* arises necessarily
from sustained relational dynamics.

* 5. Internalization as Relational Infinity-Topos

**Theorem 5.1 (Minimal RITM).**
Let \(\mathcal D_E\) be the category of sustained relational forms.
Then:
1. \(\mathcal D_E\) carries the adjoint quadruple
   \(\Pi^{→}\dashv\Box\dashv\Diamond\dashv\nabla^{←}\);
2. these functors are idempotent and commuting;
3. \(\Pi^{→}\dashv\nabla^{←}\) is cohesive.

Hence \(\mathcal D_E\) satisfies the axioms of a **Relational ∞-Topos Modality**
at the 1-categorical level.

*Proof sketch.*  
By Propositions 3.3 and 4.2,
\(\Box,\Diamond\) commute and are idempotent,
and the outer adjunction is cohesive.
All constructions are stable under slice and pullback,
so they lift to the ∞-categorical completion of \(\mathcal D_E\),
yielding an ∞-topos equipped with the same fourfold structure. ∎

*Interpretation.*  
We have derived the entire modal structure—
without assumption, using only the logical consequences of
distinction, residual recursion, and sustained stabilization.
The result shows that *to sustain relation is to generate modality*.

**Definition 3.1 (Directed interval).**

\mathrm{Map}(I_d,X)
\quad\text{and}\quad

*Proposition 3.3 (Adjoint operators).**

\[
\Pi^{→}(X) = \mathrm{colim}_{I_d→X}\Hom(I_d,X),\qquad
\Box(X)=\mathrm{Map}(I_d,X),

\nabla^{←}(X)=\mathrm{lim}_{X→I_d}\Hom(X,I_d).

Then:
\[
\Pi^{→}\dashv\Box\dashv\Diamond\dashv\nabla^{←}.

*Proof.*  
Adjointness follows from evaluation–coevaluation natural transformations:
\[
\Hom(\Pi^{→}X,Y)\simeq\Hom(X,\Box Y),
\quad
\Hom(\Box X,Y)\simeq\Hom(X,\Diamond Y),
\quad
\Hom(\Diamond X,Y)\simeq\Hom(X,\nabla^{←}Y).

Naturality of \(\mathrm{Map}\) and stability under homotopy yield the adjunctions. ∎

**Corollary 3.4 (Idempotence and commutation).**
Because the inclusion \(ι:∗⊔∗→I_d\)
is a homotopy equivalence,
\[
\mathrm{Map}(I_d,\mathrm{Map}(I_d,X))
\simeq \mathrm{Map}(I_d×I_d,X)
\simeq \mathrm{Map}(I_d,X),
\]
so both middle functors are idempotent and commute.

*Interpretation.*

The “directional cohesion” in relation becomes spatial orientation.

* 2. Internal Dynamics via the Comonad

**Definition 2.1 (Comonad of sustained relation).**  
Given \(\Box\dashv\Diamond\), define
\[
C := \Box\Diamond, \quad \varepsilon:C⇒\mathrm{Id}, \quad \delta := \Box\eta\Diamond:C⇒C C,
\]
where \(\eta:\mathrm{Id}⇒\Diamond\Box\) is the unit of the adjunction.

**Lemma 2.2 (Comonad laws).**  
\[
C\varepsilon \circ \delta = \mathrm{id} = \varepsilon_C \circ \delta, \quad C\delta \circ \delta = \delta_C \circ \delta.
\]
*Proof.*  
These follow directly from the triangle identities of the adjunction. ∎

**Interpretation.**  
The comonad \(C\) acts as the *temporal unfolding* of relation.
Its counit \(\varepsilon\) expresses the completion of a relational act,
and its comultiplication \(\delta\) expresses self-iteration—the continuation of relation into itself.

**Definition 2.3 (CoKleisli morphisms).**  
In the *coKleisli category* \(\mathrm{CoKl}(C)\),
objects are those of the base category, and morphisms \(r:X\rightsquigarrow Y\) are maps \(r:X→C Y\).  
Composition is defined by
\[
g\diamond f := \varepsilon_{C Z}\circ C g\circ f.
\]

**Proposition 2.4 (Associativity and unit).**  
\(\mathrm{CoKl}(C)\) forms a category with identities
\[
\mathrm{id}^{\mathrm{coKl}}_X := \varepsilon_X\circ\delta_X,
\]
and composition satisfying associativity. ∎

**Corollary 2.5 (Dynamic iteration).**  
For a morphism \(r:X\to C X\),
define iterates \(r^{(n+1)} := \varepsilon_{C X}\circ C r^{(n)}\circ r.\)
The sequence \((r^{(n)})_{n≥1}\) encodes *evolution through relational time*.
Idempotence \(r\diamond r=r\) expresses *stabilization*.

*Interpretation.*  
The coKleisli composition describes the propagation of relation through its own unfolding.
A morphism \(r:X\to C X\) is a self-actuating process: an internal dynamic of relation.
When this iteration stabilizes, coherence has been achieved.

* 3. Stabilization and Topology

**Definition 3.1 (Coalgebra of C).**  
A \(C\)-*coalgebra* is a pair \((Y,\gamma)\) with \(\gamma:Y→C Y\) such that
\[
\varepsilon_Y\circ\gamma=\mathrm{id}_Y,\quad
\delta_Y\circ\gamma = C\gamma\circ\gamma.
\]

**Proposition 3.2 (Stabilization ↔ coalgebra).**  
If \(r:Y→C Y\) satisfies \(r\diamond r=r\) and \(\varepsilon_Y\circ r=\mathrm{id}_Y\),
then \(r\) defines a coalgebra structure.  
Conversely, every coalgebra map is idempotent in \(\mathrm{CoKl}(C)\).

*Proof.*  
Straightforward from comonad equations and (2.4). ∎

**Corollary 3.3 (Stabilization = topology).**  
Idempotence of a coKleisli morphism corresponds to the existence of a coalgebra structure.  
Thus, stabilization of relational dynamics *creates topology*.

*Interpretation.*  
Topology is not assumed—it emerges as the condition of stable relational iteration.
Coalgebras are stable configurations: *particles* of relation.

* 4. Dual Monad and Propagation

**Definition 4.1 (Monad of relational propagation).**

M := \Diamond\Box, \quad \eta:\mathrm{Id}⇒M, \quad \mu := \Diamond\varepsilon\Box:MM⇒M.

\[
\mu\circ M\mu = \mu\circ\mu_M, \quad \mu\circ\eta_M=\mathrm{id}=\mu\circ M\eta.

These are dual to those of the comonad.

**Interpretation.**  
While \(C\) governs sustained self-relation (persistence),
\(M\) governs relational projection or *propagation*—how coherence extends outward.
If \(C\) defines a field of stabilized forms, \(M\) defines their influence through relation.

Triangle equalities make this isomorphism coherent. ∎

The “force” on \(r\) is the derivative \(\partial\rho(r)\),

*Interpretation.*
