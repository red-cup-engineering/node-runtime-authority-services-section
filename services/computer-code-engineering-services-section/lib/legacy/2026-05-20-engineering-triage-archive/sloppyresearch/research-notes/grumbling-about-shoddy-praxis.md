In a Discord for decolonial Marxism I'm in, there's someone who announced a software they claimed a bunch of things about an Infrastructure-as-Code deployment for Fossil SCM; the claims were mostly related to protecting radical movements from threats.

They showed a video, made by NotebookLM, that explained the threat, and then explained how Fossil's provenance control worked, as though detailed and centralized provenance and change records aren't… exceptionally useful in current legal repression of political movements, around the globe.

And that's kind of my grumble: like, this person clearly went to AI with some legitimate needs for a local orgization, and got a legitimate solution to the problem they laid out.

The issue is, they then saw this as some MVP and invented up a bunch of use-cases to build a market, and the construction of those use-cases show a completely misunderstanding of the software's actual use.

Another facet is the conflation with any of that functionality with what the person said was actually the project's purpose, "its more to capture and version control ideological broad conversations and document a public facing organizations official statements and communications while allowing participation from 'the public'."

So, the production of commodity-form ideology, sold as a safe space to plan radical action…

It's really hard not to see this as affirming what I've been theorizing: californication as the mode of existing that liberal subjects curve to.

Another thing is that in the context of the space, a lot of these technical questions about their software might seem like their answers are opinions, especially from how they write, and that's just not true.

For example, they claimed it was "auditable by open source," "open source for auditability," but when I asked for the git repo, I got told it's "under development."

It's the equivalent of claiming that your bakery complies with food safety standards and lets health inspectors inspect things… when you don't, but maybe down the line you will.

I feel like I'm literally too exhausted by this cadence of behavior from folk across the Left right now to even give a coherent complaint about it, but I wanted to write down something so I didn't forget about it entirely, because… it is straight-up weird that people are coming into the theory channel of a decolonial Marxist Discord server to promote their book club's forum instance as a model for how to let radicals safely communicate. Like I'm picturing some American in 2012 learning about phpBB and thinking they finally can start an American color revolution. (And, well, we did get Discourse in 2013, I'm remembering… fuck.)
