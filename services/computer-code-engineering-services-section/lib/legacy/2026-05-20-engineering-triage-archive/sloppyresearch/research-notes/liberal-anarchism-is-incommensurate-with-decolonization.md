[claims](id:e2e604d0-d036-4ea5-83aa-cd29d541ca67)  
- Given that [liberal anarchism](id:24aa5028-0861-4fa5-b938-5f027fa965bb), that is, anarchism of the liberal subject, requires itself be functional within [liberalism](id:5a898ac0-0046-4074-b0d5-ed6b60aac677) and thus a part of [liberal worlding](id:48a095dc-a384-460d-bb06-6090fdaf621a), it must maintain [compatibility](id:b75989d1-adc1-4793-b7ba-09ee9a480172) with liberalism.
- Given the [lineage of liberal anarchism](id:0d6e5a07-e2b9-439b-a7bb-4b184064bca2) is [deviation](id:24904a48-edd4-4b22-b5f5-4905294169a7) of the [lineage of liberal individualism](id:3c853919-68ad-45e0-a415-2e9d5610e1d1), its [a priori determinations](id:e3f39153-5c1f-4999-ba28-34407c15ba64) may be [derived](id:fc7d1ea7-e967-46b9-9fdf-1b15cbba3b0f) from that lineage.
