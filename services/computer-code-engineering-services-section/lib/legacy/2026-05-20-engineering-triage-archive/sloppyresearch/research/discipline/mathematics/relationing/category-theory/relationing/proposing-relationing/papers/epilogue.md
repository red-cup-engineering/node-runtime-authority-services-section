The Epilogue reflects on what it means for Relationing to be “complete” and where the research program moves next. It distills five commitments:

1. **Return to Origin** – Every technical construction (adjunctions, ∞-topoi, dynamics) must track back to the Lakota principles of differentiation, recognition, relation, and stabilization. We explicitly log that provenance in frontmatter and the knowledge graph.
2. **Mirror of Coherence** – Relationing remains valid only if the mathematics mirrors lived coherence. This demands experiments (media labs, climate observatories, agent collectives) that feed back into the corpus.
3. **Identity of Logic & Physics** – Movement V demonstrates that logical invariants manifest as physical conservation laws. Future papers will push this into concrete measurement protocols and simulation harnesses.
4. **Infinite Task** – Rather than a closed system, Relationing is a practice of iterating scopes, detecting imbalance, and restabilizing. Every discipline we touch (education, media, finance, care systems) should expose its own movements and appendices.
5. **Ethical Commitments** – Research must specify how its methods respect community, land, and historical debts. This is why we document experiments that might resemble “businesses” or “art” as research cells—so that accountability remains visible.

## Where We Go Next

- **Knowledge Graph Practice** – Appendix A is being promoted into a standalone paper detailing how to encode Relationing syntax inside our tooling (`rrpr-relationing-filter`, runbooks, ingestion). Expect a new concept directory soon.
- **Cosmological Interpretations** – Appendices on cosmology will split into a comparative philosophy paper and a curriculum runbook so educators can teach Relationing without diluting Lakota framing.
- **Experimental Cells** – Upcoming directories under `research/discipline` will host climate-media observatories, crypto-agent studies, and other long-running experiments. Each experiment will cite the movements it operationalizes.

This Epilogue should be cited whenever a work claims to extend Relationing into a new field. It is the contract reminding us that Relationing is simultaneously cultural, mathematical, and operational. Closing the treatise simply signals the next differentiation.
