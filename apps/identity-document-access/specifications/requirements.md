# Identity document access: requirements

The first consumer is Morgan Sennhauser's personal civilianization service. Its current owner-held case, recovered correspondence, rejected DVS draft and October 26 request are the work intake. The problem is establishing a feasible evidence path when conventional identification is absent, rather than enumerating application requirements. This implementation does not claim government acceptance or completion of the customer's objective.

1. Keep customer facts and records in their personal service. The reusable engine contains no Morgan-specific facts, credential assumptions, jurisdiction rules or contact destinations.
2. Admit domain input using the existing JSON Schema evaluator (Draft 2020-12). Public assertions use native SignCell source statements; conflicting evidence uses Data's FOUR readings. A consumer may pin a studied statement's native address; changed assertion content or source identity must block that guard until reviewed. Do not implement substitute identity, schema or evidence algebra.
3. Distinguish possession, proposed acquisition, published procedure and receiver decision. A draft, delivery failure, invitation or published waiver cannot become a filed application, denial, appointment, approval or received ID.
4. Assess AND prerequisites and alternative acquisition paths from observed evidence. Expose circular dependencies, unavailable prerequisites, unknown/contested assertions and discretionary receiver decisions. Planning never creates possession or a receipt.
5. Every public prerequisite assertion names an exact source and passage. Superseded, future-dated, integrity-failed and out-of-review sources cannot support an executable recommendation. Preserve their history. Compare source versions by the claims and actions affected, rather than invalidating unrelated facts.
6. Generate a variance obligation matrix containing separate arguments, exhibits, provenance, recovery attempts and unresolved questions. An argument must not count as acceptance by the decision-maker.
7. Produce research tasks with a specific missing fact or relationship, retrieval method, already checked evidence, stopping condition and fallback. Do not make generic agency outreach the default remedy for every unknown.
8. Offer explicit counterfactual assessment without mutating the case. Additional clients must be isolated by invocation. Case authorization can disable a route but cannot be inferred from a planned action.
9. Expose reusable functions and a local CLI, with an actual personal-service caller and counterexample tests. Local source operation, installed dependencies, government action and physical credential receipt remain distinct receiving events.

External contracts: [JSON Schema Draft 2020-12](https://json-schema.org/draft/2020-12), the exact exported native dependencies in package.json, and jurisdiction-specific primary sources supplied by the consumer. The engine does not declare new legal rules or a universal sufficiency threshold.
