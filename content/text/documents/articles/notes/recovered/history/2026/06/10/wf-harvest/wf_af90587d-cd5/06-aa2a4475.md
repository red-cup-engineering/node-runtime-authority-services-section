# Boundary design for @emsenn/meme-topology — service/daemon persona

Designed for a long-lived settling daemon: gated admission of witnesses over an address space, append-only persistence, repeated re-settling, observer-relative section serving over HTTP/SSE. Grounded in /home/emsenn/561-group/lib/meme-topology/src/meme-topology.mts and /home/emsenn/561-group/lib/meme-topology-addressing/src/index.mts.

---

## 1. Public entrypoint structure: curated root + five subpaths, not 13, not 22

**Decision: a curated root barrel plus exactly five subpath exports — `.`, `./charge`, `./readings`, `./serialization`, `./arithmetic`, `./internal`.** Kill the 22-module star-export and the half-finished 13-subpath map.

Argument from the daemon's life:

- A daemon imports in exactly three moods: **admission** (build witnesses, gate them), **settling** (induce/extend, classify regime), **serving** (read sections, partition charges, serialize). The current 13 subpaths slice by *mathematical construction layer* (./predication-fiber, ./bilattice-linear-logic), which is the library author's view, not any consumer's. Nine modules — including settling, cohomology, and the fixed-point readings a daemon serves every request from — have no subpath at all, so the subpath map actively lies about the boundary.
- The root barrel currently exports ~850 flat names including `judgment`, `divide`, `present`, `rank`, `Witness`, `Matrix`. A daemon also imports an HTTP framework, a logger, and addressing; flat generic names guarantee collision and mis-import across 75 packages. The tests already treat the barrel as the boundary (every test imports `./index.mjs`), so curating the barrel is curating the spec.
- One `./internal` subpath (no semver promise, documented as such) holds everything in §3, so nothing is deleted — the closure engine, stage builders, and sub-carrier constructors remain reachable for the rare consumer (e.g., a sharded daemon driving per-fiber settling by hand) without polluting the contract.

Root `.` carries the keystone and judgment vocabulary (§2 groups A–D). `./charge` carries the bilattice value algebra (hot path for fold-style consumers like swarm memory; small, dependency-free, importable without pulling the closure engine's types). `./readings` carries section/partition/count reads (what an HTTP layer imports — it should never see `induce`). `./serialization` is new (§4). `./arithmetic` carries exact-number + spectral + substitution-dynamics — real, tested capability, but no daemon request path touches it; separating it keeps the core teachable.

Within the root, adopt the namespaced-reexport pattern for the mid-tier (`export * as settling`, `export * as relation`, `export * as theory`) so kind paths and import paths agree and the flat-name collision surface dies.

## 2. Public vocabulary, by category (old → new)

Naming rules applied: judgment unions end in `Judgment`, halves in `Witness`/`Obstruction`; `Witness` is reserved for judgment-affirmatives; carriers named `*Carrier` iff kind ends `.carrier`; kind strings move to `:witness` / `-obstruction` per Three Dots and a Dash (mechanical, two sites per kind).

## 3. What becomes internal (`./internal`), and why

1. **All of carrier-coincidence**, especially the 9 `*Holds` booleans — tests pointedly reimplement two of them from carriers (math.test.mts:272-278) rather than import them; the module is derived plumbing over identity judgments, and the booleans are the house-discipline breach most likely to be cargo-culted by 75 downstream packages.
2. **Stage/sub-carrier constructor zoos** — settling's 13 stage builders (predication-settling.mts:187-298), the 6+6 intermediate constructors in valuation-consistency and fixed-point-gap, firing's 11 nullary type factories, meme-topology-fixed-point-reading's 5 information assemblers. Their *outputs* are contract (asserted as fields of parent carriers in tests); the constructors are `induce`'s own assembly line. A daemon never builds a `settlingLimitStageInput` by hand — and if a sharded daemon someday does, `./internal` is exactly where it should knowingly reach.
3. **Closure-engine plumbing** — `closureApproximant`, `closureOrbitOfProcess`, `stabilizingSup`, `closureSearchBlock(s)`, `closureStabilizationSearchHorizon`, `showOrdinal`, the omega-ordinal family; plus predication-valuation-closure's `lastValuation`, `sameMeme`, `sameValuation`, `transportValuationAlongPredicationGenerators`, and the entire untested truth-side closure pipeline and modal raise/lower/necessitate operators. The modal operators are genuinely unspecified (zero tests except `possibilizeKnowledge`); publishing unspecified capability to 75 packages is how you get accidental contracts. Explicitly documented: "modal operators are internal pending spec" — answering the program-processor's `knowledge.necessitate` demand with a deliberate no-for-now rather than silence.
4. **Charge introspection battery** — `ChargePair`, `compareKnowledge`, `present`, `valueOf`, `chargeSideName`, the `is*` validators except `isCharge`, carrier-level join/meet/negate/conflate duplicates.
5. **`Mark`/`Token`/`Claim` classes**, `cellOf`, typed-theory's nullary morphism constructors, `fiberFromAlgebra`, the Frobenius bare-boolean trio (until carrier-ized), `relationReadAbsence` constructor.

Everything internal keeps working — `induce` composes it — but the semver contract and the docs cover only §2.

## 4. What is MISSING for a daemon — specific signatures

These are the additions without which a service cannot be built on this library at all. Ranked.

**4a. Polarized, provenanced admission.** Today `induce` seeds every cell at `witnessedPotential(c.subject)` (meme-topology.mts:42): the daemon can never reach `active` or `stable` through the keystone — the library's central promise (stable = held contradiction) is unreachable from its front door, and the evidence slot holds the cell's own subject, which is audit-useless. Smallest sufficient change:

```ts
export type Polarity = "affirm" | "deny";
export interface Attestation {
  readonly kind: "predication.fiber.attestation";
  readonly predication: Cell | SpecializationMorphism;
  readonly polarity: Polarity;            // default "affirm" preserves current behavior
  readonly provenance?: unknown;          // opaque; kernel never inspects, carries monotonically
}
export function attest(predication: Cell | SpecializationMorphism): Attestation; // affirm, no provenance — today's witnessCarrier
export function attestWith(
  predication: Cell | SpecializationMorphism,
  polarity: Polarity,
  provenance?: unknown,
): Attestation;
```
`induce` seeds affirm→potential-side, deny→active-side; opposing attestations on one cell join (knowledge) to `stable` — exactly the bilattice already computes (`joinKnowledgeValue(potential, active) === stable`, math.test.mts:475). Round-trips with the memetics TraceDatom five-tuple.

**4b. Incremental settling.** `induce` is batch and eager (4 extrema + trace + cohomology + theory + proofs per call). A daemon admitting a stream cannot pay full recompute per witness. Evidence monotonicity makes warm restart sound:

```ts
export function extend(
  carrier: MemeTopologyCarrier,
  attestations: readonly Attestation[],
): MemeTopologyCarrier;
// Law: extend(induce(a), b) ≡ induce([...a, ...b])  (deep-equal carriers; property-test this)
// Implementation freedom: re-close from carrier's knowledge-least seeded with the delta.
```
Plus laziness for the expensive optional fields — either thunked fields or a split:
```ts
export function induceCore(attestations: readonly Attestation[]): MemeTopologyCoreCarrier;
// fixedPoints + readings + settling only
export function elaborate(core: MemeTopologyCoreCarrier): MemeTopologyCarrier;
// adds settlingFirstCohomology, theory, proofs
```
A daemon serves from core on the hot path and elaborates on audit request.

**4c. Section reads at value level.** The only point read takes a constructed Cell; enumeration requires the fragile chain `reading.extremum.sheaf.section.fibers[].assignments[]` (meme-topology.mts:100). Every domain (SHP, web-swarm-gui, news, ledger, language-generation) re-derives the same four-way partition. Ship once, in `./readings`:

```ts
export interface SectionEntry {
  readonly kind: "memeTopology.section.entry";
  readonly cell: Cell;
  readonly charge: ChargeCarrier;
  readonly attestations: readonly Attestation[]; // provenance for audit/why reads
}
export interface SectionCarrier {
  readonly kind: "memeTopology.section.carrier";
  readonly order: OrderName; readonly bound: BoundName;
  readonly entries: readonly SectionEntry[];
}
export function sectionOf(carrier: MemeTopologyCarrier, order: OrderName, bound: BoundName): SectionCarrier;
export function predicationsFor(section: SectionCarrier, subject: Mark): readonly SectionEntry[];
export function subjectsFor(section: SectionCarrier, predicate: Mark): readonly SectionEntry[];
export function chargeAtValues(section: SectionCarrier, subject: Mark, predicate: Mark, object?: Mark): ChargeCarrier; // absurd when absent — absence is data
export interface SectionPartitionCarrier {
  readonly kind: "memeTopology.section.partition";
  readonly affirmed: readonly SectionEntry[];   // active
  readonly denied: readonly SectionEntry[];      // (truth-negative reading)
  readonly held: readonly SectionEntry[];        // stable — contradictions, never errors
  readonly undetermined: readonly SectionEntry[];// absurd/potential residue
}
export function partitionSection(section: SectionCarrier): SectionPartitionCarrier;
export interface TopologyVitalsCarrier { // monitoring: cheap counts, no fiber-walking
  readonly kind: "memeTopology.vitals";
  readonly cells: Natural; readonly contradictions: Natural; readonly residue: Natural;
  readonly regime: ClosureRegime; readonly quiesced: FixedPointJudgment;
}
export function topologyVitals(carrier: MemeTopologyCarrier): TopologyVitalsCarrier;
```

**4d. Serialization seam (`./serialization`), in concert with addressing.** Two hard breaks today: addressing's `termKey` throws on bigint (addressing index.mts:16) while exact-number is bigint-backed, and there is no canonical address form for Mark/Token/Claim/Cell. Nothing crosses a process boundary. Needed:

```ts
export function dehydrateAttestation(a: Attestation): JsonValue;   // bigints as decimal strings
export function rehydrateAttestation(v: JsonValue): Attestation;   // throws TypeError on malformed (allowed)
export function dehydrateSection(s: SectionCarrier): JsonValue;
export function rehydrateSection(v: JsonValue): SectionCarrier;
export function cellTerm(cell: Cell): AddressTerm;                 // canonical [subjectKey, predicateKey, objectKey] for addressing.termKey / logPath
export function attestationTerm(a: Attestation): AddressTerm;
export function sectionContentKey(s: SectionCarrier): string;      // canonical-serialization digest → ETag/sync
```
The daemon's whole persistence story is then: `logPath(root, cellTerm(cell))` JSONL append of `dehydrateAttestation`, replay with `rehydrate` + `induce`/`extend` on boot. Carriers themselves never cross the wire — sections and attestations do; that boundary should be stated, not implied.

**4e. Named-operation registry.** Program-processor and MPTE interpret named morphisms; the boundary needs an enumerable, frozen `operations: ReadonlyMap<string, (…)…>` (or a const-object) keyed by kind-grammar strings, covering exactly the §2 surface — so interpreters target the boundary without reflection and the registry doubles as the machine-readable contract.

## 5. Consumer contract (README head)

> @emsenn/meme-topology turns attestations — affirmed or denied predications with opaque provenance — into a frozen, pure, replayable topology: `induce(attestations)` (or `extend(carrier, delta)`; evidence is monotone, so extension and re-induction agree) settles every cell to one of four charge values, where **stable is a held contradiction, never an error**, and the knowledge and truth orders never mix. Every judgment returns a witness-or-obstruction carrier — no booleans, no domain exceptions; throws mean malformed input only. Read results through sections (`sectionOf`, `partitionSection`, `predicationsFor`, `topologyVitals`), never by walking carrier internals; sections and attestations are the serialization boundary (dehydrate/rehydrate + addressing terms), carriers are not. All arithmetic is exact, all carriers are frozen, and kind strings follow Three Dots and a Dash (`.` generated-from, `:` witnesses, `-` obstructs, `_` escape). Anything under `./internal` is the engine, not the contract.
